# Leadership (the Council, elders and leaders)

Leadership lists the church's leadership groups – the Church Council, the elders, ministry leaders, or whatever your church
has – and who serves on each, with their **role** (Chair, Secretary, Treasurer…), their **title** (Churchwarden, Elder…) and
their **portfolios** (Finance, Buildings, Youth…). The church switches it on in Settings → Apps (it's off until then).

## Who is on the Church Council?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Who are the elders? Who are the churchwardens? Who looks after finance? Who is the treasurer?

Open **Leadership**. Each group lists the people who serve on it, with their role, title and what they look after. Some
groups are shown only to their own members – if you don't see a group, it is one of those.

## How do I add a leadership group?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Set up the Council. Add the elders. Create a committee.

On **Leadership**, type the group's name (e.g. *Church Council*) under **Add a leadership group** and tap **Add**. Then add
its people: type part of a name in the group's box, pick the person, and fill in their role, title, portfolios and the
dates they serve. Only the name is needed; leave the rest empty if it doesn't apply.

## How do I change someone's role, title or portfolios?
For: Administrators, Pastors

On **Leadership**, tap the pencil next to the person. A person can have one role and one title, and several portfolios –
separate them with commas (*Finance, Buildings*).

## Someone's time on the Council has ended. What do I do?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Remove someone from the Council. A warden stepped down. End of term.

Tap the **✕** next to the person, check the date, and choose **Move to “Served before”**. They stay on the group's record
under **Served before**, with their role and dates, so the church keeps its history. If they were added by mistake, choose
**Added by mistake – remove** instead.

## Who can see a leadership group?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Hide a group. Make a group private.

Everyone who signs in, unless you change it: tap the pencil next to the group's name and untick **Everyone who signs in can
see this group**. Then only its own members, Administrators and Pastors see it. Only names, roles, titles and portfolios
are shown – never contact details.

## What do leadership groups have to do with the Archive?
For: Administrators

A record in the Archive can be **for a leadership group** – for example the Council's minutes. The people on that group
can then read it. When someone joins or leaves the group, what they can read changes with it. See [archive.md](archive.md).
