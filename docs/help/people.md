# People (for staff)

What you can see and change in **People** depends on your role: Administrators and Pastors can change people;
Roster coordinators see names and contact details; Children's ministry sees children, guardians and medical notes. Members
see the **Directory** (people who agreed to be in the church directory).

## How do I find someone?
For: Everyone with People
Also asked: Look up a phone number. Search for a family.

Type in **Search people** – a name, household, email or phone. Every word you type must appear, in any order ("andr pi"
finds Andrew Pike). **Sort by** first name, known-as name or surname. Members use the **Directory**.

## How do I add a new person?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: New member. Add a newcomer. Add a child.

Tap **Add person**. Fill in the shaded boxes (first and last name, category, marital status – or **Not known** – and their
household, or **+ New household…** if they live on their own). Choose whether they're an adult or a **Child (under 18)** in
the household. Only add what the church needs, and ask for their consent when you add them. Church Hub warns you as you
type if a similar person is already there.

## How do I change someone's details?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Open the person and tap **Edit details**. Save when you're done.

## How do I give someone access to Church Hub?
For: Administrators (Pastors if allowed)
Also asked: Let someone sign in. Make someone a roster coordinator. Change someone's role.

Open the person → **Edit details** → **App access**, and choose what they can see and do: Administrator, Pastor, Roster
coordinator, Children's ministry, Member (Basic access) or **No access**. They sign in with Google or an email link using
the email address on their record. People who share an email address share one sign-in.

## How do I add a child's parents, guardians, and who may collect them?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Children's ministry
Also asked: Who can fetch this child? Court order. Guardian consent.

Open the child → **Parents, guardians and collection** → **Add a parent, guardian or collector**. Choose the person (or
type the name and phone of someone not in Church Hub), the relationship, and tick **Can give consent**, **Primary contact**
and/or **May collect**. **Add someone who may not collect** lists people who are not allowed to collect the child (shown with
a red bar; every view is logged). Links made automatically need to be confirmed – they're on your Home **To do** list.

## Where do I record a child's allergies or medical notes?
For: Children's ministry
Also asked: Allergies. Medical alert.

Open the child → **Medical and allergies** → **Edit medical notes**. Keep it short and factual – what leaders need for
safety. Only Children's ministry can see these. A parent or guardian needs to have given consent for medical notes.

## How do households work?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Families. Link a child to two homes. Separated parents.

A household is who lives together. Choose it in **Edit details** (**Household**). A child of separated parents can be in
both: **Also lives in another household**. The **Households** page lists them; a household is named after its adults unless
you give it your own name. Couples are worked out from each household – confirm them under **Households → Couples to
confirm**.

## How do I find and merge duplicates?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Someone is in twice. Merge two people.

**People → Find duplicates** lists people who are probably or possibly the same. Choose **Same person: fix…** to keep one
record (gaps are filled, nothing is overwritten) or **Not the same person**. The sensitivity slider (Administrators) makes
it find more or fewer.

## How do I do an advanced search or download a list?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Roster coordinators, Children's ministry
Also asked: List of everyone with a birthday this month. Export to Excel. Filter people.

**People → Advanced search**: add filters (field, how to compare, value), choose **all** / **any** or your own logic, pick
the columns, then **Download (CSV)** (opens in Excel). You only see fields your role may see. Searches can't be
saved yet.

## Can I just type or say what I'm looking for?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Roster coordinators, Children's ministry
Also asked: Search in plain English. Voice search. Ask a question about people.

Yes – at the top of **People → Advanced search**, use **Ask in your own words**. Type (or tap the microphone and say) what
you want, in English, then tap **Ask**. For example: *Church Partners who are men, with birthdays in April*, *women over 60
who live in Howick*, *first names beginning with M*, *Church Partners whose wives' names begin with A*, *Church Partners who
don't belong to a care group*, *people without an ID number*, or *10am services between 12 November
and 2 February that don't have all positions filled*. Church Hub fills in the filters and shows what it understood, and any
words it didn't – you can then change the filters by hand. It only searches what your role may see.
If the church has switched on AI (Settings → Apps), the question is understood by Google's Gemini AI and it says
**Understood by AI as**; only your question is sent, never the church's records. Don't type private matters into it.

## How do I change many people at once?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Bulk update. Change category for everyone.

In **Advanced search**, tick people in the results and choose **Change selected…** (marital status, category, gender,
Church Partner dates, or filling gaps). **Undo** puts everyone back.

## Someone has died. What do I do?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Open the person → **Record a death**: the date, the funeral or memorial (if known), an optional pastoral note, and whether
to change their spouse to Widowed. They stay on the records as *Deceased* and leave the directory. Then **Finish: take off
rosters, teams, leave and sign-in** (Administrators; it's on the To do list). Pastors get "Care for the family of …" on
their to-do list for 8 weeks. Made a mistake? **Recorded by mistake?** undoes it.

## Someone has left the church.
For: Administrators, Pastors

Open them → **Edit details** → **Mark as left** (at the top; tap again to confirm). They're hidden from lists, and their history is
kept. For a test person or someone added by mistake, use **Move to Bin** instead – they can be restored for 30 days.

## Where are pastoral notes?
For: Pastors

On the person's page → **Pastoral notes** → **Add a note**. Only Pastors can see them, and views are logged.
(**Church office notes** are for Administrators and Pastors.)

## How do I see someone's ID number?
For: Administrators

On the person's page, tap **Show** next to the ID number. Every view is logged. The person can see their own.

## Someone asked to change their family details. Where do I check it?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Changes people asked for.

On your Home **To do** list (*… changes to family details to check*), which opens **Changes people asked for**. **Yes, make these changes** saves the marital status and anniversary; then
add the spouse and children to their household and tap **Done**. **Don't change** sends a reply they'll see.
