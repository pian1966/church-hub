# Settings and setup (for Administrators)

Only Administrators can change settings, unless they let Pastors (see *What Pastors can do*). Use **Search settings**
at the top of Settings to jump to one.

## How do I switch an app on or off?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Turn on midweek groups / Bible studies. Hide Leave. Switch off care groups.

**Settings → Apps**. Switching an app off hides it; nothing is deleted. Midweek groups start off until you switch them on.

## How do I change the church's name, country or colour?
For: Administrators

**Settings → Church**: church name, **Country** (sets provinces/states, postcodes, phone numbers and the privacy law),
and the church's colour (**Save the church's colour**) – everyone sees it.

## How do I use our own church words (e.g. Rector, Parishioner)?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Change "Church Partner" to "Member". Rename Pastor. Terminology.

**Settings → Terminology (Church Words)**. Choose your **Kind of church** (General, Anglican, Catholic, Methodist,
Presbyterian/Reformed, Baptist, Pentecostal, Lutheran) to fill in its words, then change any word. **Save wording**. The
screens use your words (in English); stored records don't change.

## How do I choose what each role can see?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Can members see phone numbers? Who can see what.

**Settings → Who can see what**. Everyone sees the names of people who agreed to share their contact details. Tick what
else each role may see (mobile, email, address, spouse). Administrators and Pastors always see everything. The **Care
groups** ticks are here too ([care-groups.md](care-groups.md)).

## What can Pastors do? Who is the Senior Pastor (Rector)?
For: Administrators

**Settings → What Pastors can do**. Pastors always see and edit everyone's details, households, children and pastoral notes;
tick what else they may do (settings, giving access, ID numbers, leave, rosters, privacy, import, translations). You can choose
one **Senior Pastor**, who gets their own ticks; the others show as Assistant Pastors. Pastors get the change the next time
they open Church Hub. Medical notes always stay with Children's ministry.

## How do I see Church Hub as another role sees it?
For: Administrators, the Senior Pastor
Also asked: View as a member. Test what a roster coordinator sees.

Tap the eye next to your name (on a phone, **More**) → **View as…** and choose a role, or **View as a carer** to see a care
group the way its carer does. You stay signed in as yourself, but see only what that role may see, and nothing can be saved.
Tap **Back to Administrator** to stop.

## How do I set up services and venues?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Add a service time. Second campus. Change the 5pm service.

**Settings → Services and venues**: each regular service's name, day, time and campus or venue (leave venue empty for the
church itself). **Services that need a roster** lists upcoming services – remove one with the bin, or use **Stop holding a
regular service…**. Special services: see [rosters.md](rosters.md).

## How do emails get sent (birthdays, anniversaries, welcome)?
For: Administrators

Church Hub suggests emails (birthdays, anniversaries, late-spouse remembrance, newcomer welcome) only to people who agreed to
email (a married couple gets one anniversary email together). Under **Send emails using** choose your email program or the
church's Gmail (opens in the browser – be signed in there with the church's Gmail account), set the **Signed by** name, and
edit the email templates. Church Hub opens each email ready to send – you press send.

## How do I bring people across from Elvanto?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Import from Elvanto. Migrate our data.

**Import → From Elvanto**: export People from Elvanto as a CSV (include Member ID – it stops people being doubled), choose
the file, match the columns and categories, check the preview, and import. Importing again updates people rather than
doubling them. If it went wrong, remove everyone brought in from Elvanto and import again. Nothing is changed in Elvanto.

## How do I bring staff leave across from Leave Manager?
For: Administrators

**Import → From Leave Manager**: in the Google Sheet use **File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**, then choose that file.
Match staff to people, check the balances in the preview, and import. Running it again updates rather than doubles, so you
can trial it and import again at switch-over.

## How do I check which version we're on?
For: Everyone

**About** shows the version and date, **What's new**, and **Check for updates**. Administrators also find the printable
**Brochures** there (for members and for other churches).
