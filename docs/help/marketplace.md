# Marketplace

The Marketplace is for buying, selling and giving away within the church. A keeper checks every listing before anyone sees it.
The church switches it on in **Settings → Apps** (it's off until then). Church Partners, staff and leaders can see and use it;
Attenders (people who only come along) don't. The church is not part of any sale. It is written to go easy on data (photos are made small), and like the rest of Church & Trellis you can still look at listings with no signal – changes wait until you're back online.

## How do I list something to sell or give away?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Sell a pram. Advertise furniture. Give something away. Post a listing. Wanted.

Open **Marketplace** and tap **List something**. Say **What are you listing?** (for example *Pine bookshelf, or a pram*), choose a
category (for example Furniture, Clothing, Vehicles, Electronics, Books or Garden), and whether it is **For sale**, **Free** or
**Wanted**. For something you have, add the **Condition** (**Like new**, **Good** or **Well used**), your **Area** and a price – or tick
**Open to offers**. For something you want, add your **Budget**. Add up to 3 photos (**Add photos** – they are made small to save
data). Tick **I accept the Marketplace rules**, then send it. It shows **Waiting for a keeper** until one approves it, and you get
a message when it's up.

## What are the Marketplace rules?
For: Everyone
Also asked: What can I sell? Is it allowed? Rules.

You can read them when you list something (**Read the Marketplace rules**). In short: only things that are yours to sell or give; no weapons, alcohol, medicines, animals
for breeding or anything illegal; and no business advertising beyond your own skills. Be honest about the condition. Your **Area**
is your suburb only – never your address. Stay safe: meet at church after a service, and don't pay before you've seen it.

## I'd like something I saw. How do I contact the seller?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Buy something. Ask about a listing. I'm interested.

Tap **I'm interested** on the listing. It goes privately to the person in **Messages** – you arrange the rest between yourselves.
Use **Search the Marketplace** or the categories to find things; **My listings** shows what you've listed.

## It's sold (or given away, or found). How do I take it down?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Mark as sold. Remove my listing. Item gone. Lower the price.

On your listing, tap **Mark as sold** (or **Mark as given away**, or **Mark as found** for something you were looking for). It
shows a stamp for a day, then goes. A listing stays up for 30 days; in its last few days you can renew it from your listing (up to twice), or change it with **Change your
listing** (a lower price stays up without being checked again).

## I'm a keeper. How do I check listings?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Keepers
Also asked: Approve a listing. Waiting for a keeper.

On **Marketplace**, listings **waiting for a keeper** are listed at the top. Tap **Approve**, or **Ask for a change** (write a short
note – it goes to the person in **Messages**), or **Decline**. Administrators choose the keepers under **More → Keepers…** and can
write the rules everyone accepts under **More → Marketplace rules…**.
