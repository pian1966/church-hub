# Care groups

A care group is a group of Church Partners looked after by one or more **carers**. Each member has a card the group
can see. The church can switch care groups off in Settings → Apps.

## Which care group am I in?
For: Members
Also asked: Who is my carer? My care group.

Open **Me** – **My care group** shows your group and its carers (and, if the church allows, everyone in it). If it says
**You're not in a care group yet.**, ask the church office. You can be in more than one group.

## What can my care group see about me?
For: Members
Also asked: Who sees my phone number? Privacy in care groups.

Your name always. If you agreed to the **Care group** consent, also a small photo, your mobile, email, address and
birthday (day and month only). Without it they see **Details not shared**. Change this under **Me → My details →
Consent → Change my choices**. Your card updates when you change your details or consent.

## How do I see the members of my group? (carers)
For: Carers
Also asked: My care group list. Phone numbers for my group.

Open **Care groups** (or your **Me** page). Your group shows each member's card. Carers always see everyone in their group.

## How do I add someone to my care group, or take someone out?
For: Carers
Also asked: A new person joined our group. Remove someone from the group.

On **Care groups**, open your group and use **Add someone to the group** – type part of their name and choose them
(people in the church directory). To remove someone, tap **Take out of the group**. Carers can't rename the group or
change the carers – ask an Administrator or Pastor.

## How do I send a message to my care group?
For: Carers
Also asked: Tell my group the meeting is cancelled. Contact my group.

Tap **Send the group a message** on your Me page or the Care groups page, write a subject and message, and send. Choose
**Only some of them** to untick people. See [messages.md](messages.md).

## How do I find someone's care group?
For: Administrators, Pastors (and roles the church allows)
Also asked: Which group is John in? Search care groups.

On **Care groups**, type in **Find a care group or a person** – part of a group's name, its area, or a person's name. Only
matching groups show, and the person is highlighted.

## How do I create, rename or delete a care group?
For: Administrators, Pastors

On **Care groups**: **Add a care group** (give it a name and, if you like, the **Areas it covers**). Open a group to
**Rename**, **Duplicate** it (with all its members, e.g. to split a group) or **Delete the group**.

## How do I put people in care groups and choose the carers?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Make someone a carer. Assign people to groups.

Open a group and use **Add someone to the group**, or open the person in **People → Edit details**, tick **Care group
member**, choose the group(s) and tick **Carer** if they lead it.

## Who isn't in a care group yet?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Church Partners without a group. Suggest groups by area.

On **Care groups**, **Church Partners not in a care group** lists them. **Suggest groups by where people live** suggests
the nearest groups (★ marks the closest). Tick people, check their group, then **Put the ticked people in their groups**.

## Who can see the care groups?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Can roster coordinators see care groups? Can members see other groups?

**Settings → Who can see what → Care groups**. You can let Roster coordinators and/or Children's ministry see every group
(names only, read-only), let Church Partners see who is in each group, and choose whether members see everyone in their
own group or only its carers.

## A member's details aren't showing on their card.
For: Carers, Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Details not shared. Card is empty.

Either they haven't agreed to the **Care group** consent (**details not shared (no consent yet)**), or they haven't signed
in since joining (**Details appear when they next sign in**). Administrators and Pastors can tap **Update everyone's
cards now**.
