# Events – what's coming up, signing up and reminders

**Events** shows what is happening at church. People sign up with one tap and are reminded **3 days and 1 day before** in
**Messages** (and by email too, if they ask and the church has switched that on). Events is switched on by an Administrator
under **Settings → Apps**.

## How do I see what's coming up, and sign up?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Put my name down. Men's breakfast. Church picnic. Register for an event.

Open **Events**. Each event shows when and where it is, and how many are coming. Tap **I'll be there**; if you're bringing
guests, say how many. You'll be reminded in **Messages** 3 days and 1 day before, and by email as well if you choose
**By email too**. Tap **Add to my calendar** to put it in your own calendar. If sign-up has closed it says
**Sign-up closed**; some events need no sign-up – just come.

## I can't come after all. What do I do?
For: Everyone

Open the event and tap **I can't come after all**. The organisers see that you're no longer coming.

## How do I put up an event?
For: Event organisers, Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Add an event. New event. Create an event.

Open **Events** and tap **New event**. Give it a title, a place (a map link is shown if it isn't at church), when it is, what
it's about and who it's for. Choose whether people sign up, when sign-up closes (leave empty to keep it open until the day),
how many guests each person may bring, and whether to **Offer reminders by email too** (needs the church's email robot –
see `docs/EVENT-EMAILS.md`). Tap **Put it up**; people see it straight away. Event organisers are chosen under
**Events → Event organisers…**.

## How do I see who's coming and write to them?
For: Event organisers, staff
Also asked: Guest list. Message everyone coming. Print the list. Sign-up report.

Open the event: **Who's coming** lists everyone and their guests. **Message everyone coming** writes to them all in
Messages. **Download the list** and **Print the list** give you the names (with cellphone numbers) – or open **Events → More →
Sign-up report** to print every event at once, one per page, or save it as a PDF. To remove someone, use **Take off the list**
(their guests go too).

## People signed up on a paper sheet. How do I add them?
For: Event organisers, staff
Also asked: Add people. Sign-up sheet. Read names from a photo.

Open the event and tap **Add people** under **Who's coming** and find each person (you can tap the microphone and say the
name). For a whole paper sheet, tap **From a sheet** and take a photo: Church & Trellis reads the names and finds each one in
People, and you check every name before anyone is added. A "+2" next to a name sets that person's guests. Pastors and
Administrators can also register someone who isn't in People yet.

## An event is cancelled or on again. What do I do?
For: Event organisers

Open the event and choose **Cancel the event…**; the people signed up are sent a message to say it is cancelled. If plans change back, tap **It's on again**.
Deleting an event asks first, and says how many sign-ups will go.
