# Archive (the church's records)

The Archives keep the church's records safely in one place: Council minutes, architectural plans, staff contracts,
annual appraisals and anything else. Open them from **Library → Archives**. The church switches on Library in
Settings → Apps (it's off until then).

## What can I see in the Archive?
For: Administrators, Council members, Librarians and members
Also asked: Where are the minutes? Can I see my contract? Why is the Archive not on my menu?

Only Administrators and Council members can open, search or manage the Archives. Librarians and other members see a
notice that access is restricted. An Administrator or Council member can see only the records their role and the
record's own settings allow:
- records marked for **everyone who signs in**;
- records for a **leadership group** you serve on (e.g. the Council's minutes, if you are on the Council);
- a record **about you** that the church has chosen to show you (e.g. your own contract).

Being the Senior Pastor, a Librarian, or the person a record is about does not grant access without Council membership
or the Administrator role.

## How do I add a record?
For: Administrators, Council members
Also asked: File the minutes. Upload a contract. Save an appraisal. Add the building plans.

In **Library → Archives**, tap **Add a record**. Give it a title, choose its category and date, check **Who can see it**, and add the
file (up to 15 MB) – or paste a link to where it is kept (e.g. Google Drive) if it is bigger. For a staff contract or
appraisal, choose the staff member it is about, and whether **they can see this record themselves**.

## Who can see a record?
For: Administrators, Council members
Also asked: Make a record private. Who can read the minutes? Keep appraisals confidential.

Each record has one of four choices under **Who can see it**:
- **Everyone who signs in** – e.g. the constitution.
- **The members of a leadership group** – e.g. the Council for its minutes. Choose the group.
- **Administrators and Pastors** – e.g. building plans.
- **Administrators and the Senior Pastor only** – staff contracts and appraisals start like this.

A new record starts with its category's choice; you can change it on each record.

## How do I file the Council's monthly minutes?
For: Administrators, Council members

First set up the Council under **Leadership**, so the Archive knows who is on it. Then add each month's minutes as a
record in **Council minutes**, with the date of the meeting. The Archive tells you which months of this year have nothing
filed yet (it starts counting from the first minutes you file).

## How do I find a record?
For: Administrators, Council members

In **Library → Archives**, type part of its title in **Search the archive**, or choose a category and a year.

## Where are the archive documents kept?
For: Administrators, Council members

Tap **Where are documents stored?** in the Archives header for the church's note about its physical and digital storage
locations. The placeholder wording is in the Archive language strings and can be replaced with the church's details.

## Can I add my own categories?
For: Administrators

Yes: **Library → Archives → Categories**. Add a category, name it, and choose who can see a new record in it. A category can only be
deleted when it has no records.

## How big can a file be?
For: Administrators, Council members

Up to 15 MB. For bigger files (large building plans, scans of many pages) keep the file somewhere like Google Drive and
paste its link into the record – remember that whoever can see the record also needs access to the file there.

## Is opening a record written down?
For: Everyone

Yes. Each time someone downloads a record that isn't for everyone, or opens its link, Church Hub writes it in the audit
log (Privacy → Audit log): who, which record and when.

## Can I download a record on my phone?
For: Everyone

In a browser, yes. In the Android app you need the newest version of the app; older versions ask you to update or use a
browser.
