# Life groups (Bible studies, small groups, midweek groups)

Life groups work like care groups, with a **leader** instead of a carer. The church switches them on in Settings → Apps
(they're off until then), and may call them something else. You can be in several.

## Which life group am I in?
For: Members
Also asked: My Bible study. My small group.

Open **Life groups** or your **Me** page (**My life groups**). If you're not in one, ask the church office or a group
leader to add you.

## How do I join a life group?
For: Members
Also asked: Find a group near me. Ask to join a Bible study.

Ask the church office or the group's leader to add you. A **Find a group** page and **Ask to join** are planned but not
built yet, and so are meeting days, times and a map of where groups meet.

## What can my life group see about me?
For: Members

Your name, and – because you agreed to be in the church directory when you became a Church Partner – your contact
details card (photo, mobile, email, address, birthday). Change your choices under **Me → My details → Consent**.

## How do I rename my group?
For: Group leaders

On **Life groups**, open your group and tap **Rename** – give it the name your group uses (e.g. *Tuesday Bible study*).
Leaders can't change who leads it.

## How do I add or remove people from my group?
For: Group leaders

Open your group: **Add someone to the group** (type part of their name – people in the church directory), or **Take out of
the group**.

## How do I message my group?
For: Group leaders

Tap **Send the group a message** on the Life groups page or your Me page. See [messages.md](messages.md).

## How do I set up life groups and leaders?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Turn on life groups. Add a Bible study. Make someone a group leader.

Switch on **Life groups** in **Settings → Apps**. Then on **Life groups** tap **Add a life group**, add people, and tap the
star next to someone to make them **Group leader**. You can also **Rename** or **Delete the group**.
