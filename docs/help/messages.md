# Messages

Messages are sent and read inside Church Hub – not by email, WhatsApp or SMS.

## Where do I read my messages?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Inbox. Did I get a message? Church notices.

Open **Messages** → **Inbox**: messages from the church to you and the groups you're in, newest first. A number on the
Messages menu (and phone tab) shows how many are unread, and they're on your Home **To do** list. Opening the inbox marks
them as read.

## Will I get a notification on my phone?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Push notifications. Email me when there's a message.

Not yet. You see new messages when you open Church Hub. Phone notifications when the app is closed, and email or SMS for
people who don't use the app, are planned.

## Can I reply to a message?
For: Everyone

Not yet – replies are planned. For now, contact the sender another way.

## Who can send messages?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Why can't I write a message?

Administrators and Pastors (to anyone), carers (to their care group), midweek group leaders (to their midweek group) and
message-group leaders (to their message group). Members can read messages but not send them.

## How do I send a message?
For: Administrators, Pastors, carers, group leaders
Also asked: Send a notice to staff. Message all Church Partners. Message one person.

1. Open **Messages → Write** (carers and leaders can also use **Send the group a message** on their Me page).
2. Choose who it's for: **Chosen people** (tick them one by one), **Staff**, **Church Partners**, a care group, a midweek group
   or a message group. To leave some people out, choose **Only some of them** and untick them.
3. Write a **Subject** and the **Message**, then **Send**. Church Hub confirms how many people it goes to.

To message one person, open their page in **People** and tap **Send a message**.
Please don't put private or medical details in a message to a group.

## Will people who join a group later see older messages?
For: Senders

No. A message goes to the people in the group when it was sent.

## Why does it say some people can't sign in yet?
For: Administrators, Pastors

They don't have Church Hub access yet, so they won't see the message until they're given access (People → Edit details →
App access). Contact them another way meanwhile.

## How do I see or delete messages I've sent?
For: Senders, Administrators

**Messages → Sent** lists what you've sent. You can delete your own messages (**Delete the message**). Administrators and
Pastors see every message sent in Church Hub and can delete any of them.

## How do I make a message group, like "Worship team leaders"?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Open **Messages → Groups** and tap **Add a message group**. Name it, add people, and mark who leads it (**Leader**) – leaders
can send messages to the group. You can **Rename** or **Delete the group** later.
