# Leave (for church staff)

Leave is for church staff (people marked as staff on their record). It works like the old Leave Manager: the same
allowances, pro-rata for new staff, carry-over rules, public holidays and church closures. Only your working days are
counted. Approved leave automatically stops you being put on the roster on those days.

## How do I apply for leave?
For: Staff
Also asked: Request leave. Book a day off. Take annual leave.

Open **Leave → My leave** and tap **Request leave**. Choose the kind of leave, the first and last day, and add a **Note
for the Administrator** if you like. Tap **Send request**. It says **Sent – the Administrator will approve it**. You can
also let the church office know.

## How much leave do I have left?
For: Staff
Also asked: My leave balance. How many days do I have?

Open **Leave → My leave**. **What you have left** shows each kind of leave with the days left and your allowance, plus any
days waiting for approval. **Your upcoming leave** lists your booked leave.

## How do I change or cancel my leave?
For: Staff
Also asked: Move my leave. I don't need the day off any more.

Open **Leave → My leave**. Under **Your upcoming leave**, tap **Change** or **Cancel** next to it (tap Cancel again to
confirm). Changing leave that's already approved sends it back to the Administrator for approval.

## Who is away this week?
For: Staff
Also asked: Staff calendar. Who's on leave? Wallchart.

Open **Leave → Who's away**. Choose **Wallchart**, **Calendar** or **List**. It shows dates only – not the kind of leave
or the reason.

## Will I lose leave I don't take?
For: Staff
Also asked: Use it or lose it. Carry over.

It depends on the church's rules for each kind of leave (set by the Administrator). Your balance on **My leave** already
takes them into account. Administrators can send "use it or lose it" reminders to staff whose leave is about to be lost.

## How do I approve or decline leave?
For: Administrators (and a stand-in approver)
Also asked: Leave requests waiting. Approve a leave request.

Open **Leave → Approvals** (requests also show on your Home **To do** list). Tap **Approve** or **Decline**. If a
request is more than the person's allowance, Church Hub warns you; you can still **Approve anyway**.

## I'm going away. Who approves leave while I'm gone?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Stand-in approver. Delegate approvals.

On **Leave → Approvals**, under **Stand-in approver**, choose a Pastor and the dates, then **Set stand-in**. They can
approve or decline leave between those dates (not their own), and their access ends by itself after the last day.

## How do I book leave for someone else?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Add leave for a staff member. Record sick leave.

Open **Leave** and tap **Book leave**, then choose the person, kind of leave and dates. To add or take away days from
someone's balance, use **Add adjustment** on the person's leave (days + or −).

## How do I add someone as staff so they can take leave?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Leave is for staff members – why can't I book leave? New staff member.

Open the person in **People**, tap **Edit details**, tick **Church staff member**, then choose their **Staff category**,
start date (**Started at the church**, used to work out a first year's allowance) and the days they work (or **Same as
their category**).

## Where do I set allowances, kinds of leave, holidays and school terms?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Change the leave allowance. Add a public holiday. Church closure. Leave year.

**Leave → Settings**: the leave year, **Staff categories and allowances**, **Kinds of leave**, **Public holidays and church
closures**, **School terms**, and **Cover rules and blocked days** (e.g. *A pastor every Sunday*). Changes apply straight
away to everyone's balances. Public holidays are South Africa's for now.

## How do I see everyone's balances or print reports?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Leave report. Payroll. Download balances.

- **Leave → Dashboard**: who's away, what's waiting, leave about to be lost, the weekly "who's away" email, and
  **Payroll: unpaid leave**.
- **Leave → Team balances**: everyone's balances – **Download team balances**, **Print: all staff summary** or **Print: all
  staff in full**. Click a name for that person's leave in detail, with **Print report** and **Download report**.

## Does leave still go into Elvanto?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Unavailable volunteers in Elvanto. The robot.

Until the church stops using Elvanto, approved leave can be added to Elvanto's Unavailable Volunteers by the robot. Switch
it on and see its results under **Leave → Elvanto**. Change leave in Church Hub, not in Elvanto – otherwise the robot can't
keep it up to date.

## Do emails go out about leave automatically?
For: Staff, Administrators

Not yet. Church Hub opens an email ready to send (for example **Let them know by email** after a decision, or the weekly
"who's away" email) – you press send in your email app. Approving from an email without signing in isn't available.
