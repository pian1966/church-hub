# Usage (for Administrators and Pastors)

**Usage** shows how many people are using Church & Trellis, who is on right now and what they use most. Only Administrators and
Pastors see it (**Usage** in the menu).

## What does the Usage page show?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: How many people are on the app. Who is using the app. Who hasn't signed in yet. Statistics.

- **Who is on the app** – the adults at the church (children are not counted): how many **Church Partners** and **Everyone else** are
  on the app, **Invited, not on yet** or **Not invited yet**.
- **On right now** – the people who opened Church & Trellis in the last 5 minutes.
- **How often it is used** – today, this week and this month, and how many people opened it each day for two weeks.
- **Growth** – how many people have started using the app, week by week, for 12 weeks.
- **Most used parts** – how often each part was opened in the last 30 days and by how many different people.
- **Which version people have**, **Busiest days** and **How people open it** (the Android app, added to the home screen, or a web browser).
- **Church Partners not on the app yet** – with an **Invite them** button that opens **People → Invitations**.

Every chart has **See the numbers** under it, which shows the same figures as a table.

## Who can see people's names on the Usage page?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Names appear only under **On right now** and **Church Partners not on the app yet**. The counts for each part never say who used what.
Members, Roster coordinators and Children's ministry cannot open Usage.

## Why does Usage say "Nothing recorded yet"?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Who is on the app comes from the church's records, so it shows straight away. The rest (who is on now, the days, the versions and the
parts) builds up as people open Church & Trellis. In the demo, made-up usage is shown so you can see what the page looks like.
