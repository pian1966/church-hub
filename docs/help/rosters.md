# Rosters

Everyone can reply to their roster turns (also on the Me page – see [me.md](me.md)). Roster coordinators and
Administrators plan the rosters. Rosters never put people in at random: families and friends who asked to serve together
are kept together, people who are away are skipped, turns are shared fairly, and every suggestion says why.

## How do I accept or decline a roster turn?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Confirm my duty. Say no to a roster. Reply to the roster.

Open **Me → Serving** (or **Rosters → My serving**). Under your upcoming turns tap **Accept** or **Can't make it**. Turns
waiting for your reply also show on your Home **To do** list.

## How do I see the whole roster?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Who is on duty this Sunday? Show the roster.

Open **Rosters**. The table shows each position down the side and the services across the top. Use **‹ Earlier** and
**Later ›** to move between dates. Colours show **Accepted**, **Waiting for a reply**, and suggestions (dashed border).
Members see their own turns under **My serving**.

## How do I fill in the roster?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Make the roster. Auto roster. Generate the roster.

1. Open **Rosters** and tap **Fill the gaps for me**.
2. Church Hub suggests people for the empty slots (dashed border). Hover or tap a suggestion to see why it chose them.
3. Tap **Keep these and ask people** – everyone suggested is asked to accept – or **Discard** to throw them away.

If some slots can't be filled, Church Hub lists them under **Why some slots are still empty** (for example, not enough
people on the team, or everyone is away). Add more people to the team, or ask someone directly.

## How do I put a particular person on the roster, or take them off?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Change who is serving. Remove someone from the roster. Mark someone as accepted.

On **Rosters**, tap the slot (the position on that date). Choose the person and **Add them**, or use **Take off the
roster**. You can also **Mark accepted** or **Mark "can't make it"** for someone who told you in person.

## How do I set up teams and positions?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Add a department. Add a position. Create a new ministry team.

Open **Rosters → Teams**.
- First time: **Set up the church's departments** adds the church's departments and positions in one click.
- **New team** adds a department (e.g. *Prayer*); **Add a position** adds a position to it (e.g. *Camera*), with how many
  people are needed per service.
- Teams can't be renamed yet.

## How do I add someone to a team?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Add a volunteer. Who can serve in each position?

Open **Rosters → Teams**, find the position and tap **Add team member**. Choose the person, how often they'd like to serve
and at which services. Staff can also do this from the person's page with **Edit volunteering**.

## Someone offered to serve. Where do I answer?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Volunteer requests. Someone wants to join the team. Offers to serve.

At the top of **Rosters → Teams** under **Offers to serve** (also on your Home **To do** list). Tap **Yes** to put them on
the team (or change how they serve), or **Not now** with a short reply they will see (e.g. *The team is full for now –
we'll ask you in the new year*).

## How do I keep people together, or apart?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators (members can ask to serve with someone)
Also asked: Married couple on the same Sunday. Parents shouldn't serve at the same time.

Open **Rosters → Serving together**.
- **Serve together**: choose at least two people and how important it is – **Always together** (rostered together or not
  at all, e.g. a family sharing a car) or **Together when possible**.
- **Keep apart**: they will never be rostered at the same service – e.g. parents taking turns with a baby.

Members can tap **Ask to serve with someone** there, or use **Serve with family or friends** on the Me page.

## How do I see who is away?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Unavailable volunteers. Blockout dates.

Open **Rosters → Away dates**. It lists the dates people can't serve. Approved leave from the Leave app is included
automatically (**From Leave**). You can **Add away dates** for someone who told you in person.

## How do I change which positions run at each service?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: No creche at the 10am. Change a service's positions.

Open **Rosters → Services**, choose the service, and tap a position to switch it on or off at that service (**Switch all
on** / **Switch all off**). Its volunteers stay on the team. Administrators can also choose the service's **Look**
(colour and picture) and **Mood** (words volunteers see).

## How do I add a special service, like Good Friday or a funeral?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Christmas service. Add an extra service. Memorial service.

Open **Rosters → Services** and tap **Add a special service**. Choose from the suggestions (the dates of Easter-based
services are worked out for you) or **Add your own**. Then **Choose its positions** – each special service only has the
departments and positions you switch on for it.

## How do I add or remove a regular service time?
For: Administrators
Also asked: We're starting a 7pm service. Add a campus or venue. Stop the evening service.

Regular services are in **Settings → Services and venues** (add a time, day, campus or venue). Upcoming dated services are
listed under **Settings → Services that need a roster**, with a bin to remove one; **Stop holding a regular service…**
removes its services from a date. See [settings.md](settings.md).
