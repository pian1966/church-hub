# Privacy and consent

Church Hub follows South Africa's privacy law (POPIA), or the law of the church's own country. The church's data is
stored in South Africa. Every person's consent is recorded for each purpose, and views of sensitive information (pastoral
notes, ID numbers, people not allowed to collect a child) are logged.

## Who can see my details?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Can other members see my phone number? Is my information safe?

- Everyone signed in sees the names (and photo) of people who agreed to be in the **church directory**. What else members
  see (mobile, email, address, spouse) is chosen by the church in Settings; members see them as pictures they can read but
  can't copy.
- Your care group sees more only if you agreed to the **Care group** consent ([care-groups.md](care-groups.md)).
- Church staff see what their role needs. Pastoral notes are for Pastors only; children's medical notes are for Children's
  ministry only.
- Read the church's **Privacy notice** (on the Me page, the sign-in screen and About).

## What is the difference between Church records, Church directory and Care group?
For: Everyone
Also asked: What does each consent mean? Contact details consent. Do I need all of them?

Tap or point at the **i** next to each one for an explanation. In short:
- **Church records** – the basic one: the church may keep your details in Church Hub at all. It shows nothing to other members.
- **Church directory** – your name and photo appear in the directory that everyone who signs in can see, with the contact
  details the church allows. Church Partners agree to this when they become Church Partners.
- **Care group** – the people in your care group may see your photo, phone, email, address and birthday.
- **Email**, **SMS** and **Photos** – news by email or SMS, and photos of you at church events.
Each is separate: you can agree to Church records without being in the directory, for example. Your own phone and email on
the Me page (**Contact details**) are just your details, not a consent.

## How do I change or withdraw my consent?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Take me out of the directory. Unsubscribe. Stop messages.

**Me → My details → Consent → Change my choices**. Withdrawing takes effect straight away.

## Can I see everything the church keeps about me, or ask for it to be deleted?
For: Everyone
Also asked: My data. Delete my information. POPIA request. Right to be forgotten.

Yes – ask the church's Information Officer (their name is in the privacy notice). They log your request in Church Hub and
answer within the time the law allows. They can export everything about you, correct it, or delete it.

## Who gives consent for a child?
For: Everyone, Children's ministry

A parent or legal guardian (someone ticked **Can give consent** on the child's page). Staff record it on the child's
profile under **Consent**, choosing who gave it.

## How do I record someone's consent?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Open the person → **Consent** → record each purpose: given or withdrawn, the date and how it was given (and for a child, which
parent or guardian). If **Record that they agreed** appears in Settings, it records the Contact details consent for existing
Church Partners, who agreed when they became Church Partners.

## What is on the Privacy page?
For: Administrators
Also asked: POPIA checklist. Information Officer. Audit log.

**Privacy** (Administrators only):
- **Overview** – a checklist (Information Officer registered, everyone has recorded consent, children's medical notes have
  guardian consent…) and the **Privacy notice** to write and save.
- **Requests** – **Log a request** (access, correction, deletion, objection), then **Export their data**, **Delete their
  information** and **Mark done**. Overdue requests are flagged.
- **Retention** – **How long we keep information**, and people who have left or died who are due for review.
- **Breaches** – **Record an incident**: what happened, who was affected, and when the regulator and people affected were
  told.
- **Audit log** – who viewed or changed sensitive information, and when.

## A printed list with phone numbers was lost. What do I do?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Data breach. Security compromise.

Go to **Privacy → Breaches → Record an incident**. Describe what happened and whose information was affected. Church Hub
shows the advice for your country's law: usually tell the regulator and the people affected as soon as possible, and record
the dates you did.

Note: this is how Church Hub helps you keep records – it isn't legal advice.
