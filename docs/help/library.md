# Library (the church's books)

The Library lists the books the church owns and shows whether each is in. Librarians add books and write down who borrows
and returns them. The church switches it on in Settings → Apps (it's off until then).

## Which books does the church have?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Is a book available? Find a book. Books by an author.

Open **Library** and type part of the title, the author or the category. Each book shows **Available** or **On loan**, and
its shelf if the church filled that in.

## How do I borrow a book?
For: Everyone

Ask a librarian – they lend it to you in Church Hub and it then appears under **My books** with the date it is due back.
Asking for a book inside Church Hub is planned but not built yet.

## Which books do I have, and when are they due?
For: Everyone

Open **Library** and look under **My books**. If a book is overdue, Home reminds you.

## Who can see what I have borrowed?
For: Everyone

Only the librarians and you. Everyone else sees only that the book is on loan. A loan is removed a year after the book
came back.

## How do I add a book?
For: Librarians
Also asked: A book was donated. We bought new books. Record where a book came from.

On **Library**, tap **Add a book**. Only the title is needed. You can also fill in the author, a category, how many copies
there are, how it was acquired (bought or donated), from whom, the date, and its shelf.

## How do I lend a book and take it back?
For: Librarians
Also asked: Check out a book. Return a book. Mark a book as returned.

Tap **Lend** next to the book, check the **Due back** date (three weeks unless you change it), type part of the borrower's
name and pick them. When the book comes back, tap **Returned** next to it under **On loan**. Overdue books are marked, and
Home tells you how many there are.

## Who are the librarians?
For: Administrators

Administrators always are. To add others, open **Library → Librarians**, type part of the person's name and pick them. They
need to be able to sign in to Church Hub.
