# Subscription

Church & Trellis asks each church for the price of one coffee a month. The price is per church, not per person: one cup a month
covers your whole church, however many people, groups and services you have. A low price doesn't mean a lesser product –
Church & Trellis competes with the best church management systems in the world, and the price is kept low so every church can
have it. Every church gets all of Church & Trellis, whichever way it takes part. These answers are for Administrators.

## What does Church & Trellis cost? Is it per person?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Price. How much is it? Cost per member. Why is it so cheap? Does a low price mean fewer features?

Open **Subscription**. You choose **a coffee** – the price of one cup where you live, in your own money – and that is your
church's **suggested price**. It's **per church, not per person**. Pick the one you'd order; if your church has plenty, you may
choose a higher one. Every way of giving gives your church all of Church & Trellis.

## How do we pay?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Pay for the year. Two months free. PayFast. Card. Instant EFT. SnapScan. Payment receipt.

On **Subscription**, choose **Who are you paying for?**, then how often: **One coffee a month** ("Stop or change whenever you
like") or **Ten coffees a year** (two coffees free when you pay for the year). Tap **Pay**, then **Continue to PayFast**. You pay
through PayFast – by card, Instant EFT or SnapScan. Church & Trellis never sees your card or your bank details, and PayFast emails
you a receipt. PayFast takes rand, so a church that pays in another currency sees the amount in rand on the next page, and its
bank changes the money. If you started a payment and aren't sure it went through, Subscription asks **Did it go through?**.

## We can't pay one month. What do we do?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Payment holiday. Skip a coffee. Late payment. We're short of money this month.

Take a **payment holiday**: skip that month's coffee, free, no questions asked. It lasts one month – the month whose coffee you
skip – and your next coffee is due a month later. A church that gives one coffee a month can take one whenever it needs, as often
as it needs: when the next coffee is due (or within a month of it), tap **Take a payment holiday** on **Subscription**. A church that
pays for the year first switches to one coffee a month (what it has already paid stays paid), then takes a holiday when the next
coffee is due. Nothing is ever locked or deleted for being late – Church & Trellis keeps working exactly as before.

## Our church can't afford a coffee. What can we do?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Free. We're a poor church. Pray instead. No money.

Choose **Pray instead** (**Free, with a promise to pray**): promise to pray for another church each week. Church & Trellis stays free
for as long as you need. No church is ever turned away for being poor. Your weekly prayer card on **Home** stays on while you pray
instead; it can be turned off once your church buys a coffee.

## Can we pay for another church, or give a gift?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Bless another church. Extra coffees. Give if we don't use it. Gift.

On **Subscription**, under **Who is it for?**, choose **Pay for another church**: pick **An under-resourced church** that has asked
for help, or **Where it's needed most** (Church & Trellis gives it to the church that has waited longest). You can also **Add a
gift** – **Extra coffees**, or any amount – to help churches that can't pay. A one-off gift is **Give a one-off gift**.

## What is the prayer circle? What is our prayer card?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Pray for another church. Weekly prayer card on Home. Make our prayer card. Keep us hidden.

Every church that uses Church & Trellis belongs to a circle of churches that pray for each other. Each week **Home** shows
everyone at your church another church to pray for – the same one for everyone that week, so you can pray for it together on
Sunday, in a prayer meeting or in a Care Group. Under **Subscription**, tap **Make our prayer card** (or **Change our prayer card**):
a photo, your town or area, and two prayer points. Look at it once a month – Home reminds the Administrator, and keeping it as
it is takes one tap. A card is the only thing about your church that is shared; never anything about your people. Pray for
things, not people by name, and leave out phone numbers, email addresses and links (Church & Trellis flags them before it saves).
A photo with people in it shows only once you tick that everyone who can be recognised has agreed.

## Is it safe for churches where Christians face danger?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Hidden prayer card. Persecution. Keep us hidden. Show our church's name.

Yes. In countries where following Jesus is dangerous, a church's card starts hidden: other churches see only "a church in" a wide
region – never its name, town, country or photo. You can turn **Keep us hidden** off yourselves; you are warned first.
