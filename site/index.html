Everything your church runs on, in one place.

The things the big church apps do, made simple enough for a volunteer to run and affordable for any congregation.

People and care

  • A directory that makes sense. Households kept together, consent recorded for each person, name badges printed on your own labels.
  • Find anyone in everyday words, typed or spoken, with optional AI search. Duplicates are spotted as you type.
  • Pastoral notes stay with the pastors, and children's medical notes with the children's ministry.

Sundays that run smoothly

  • Rosters that keep families together and skip the Sundays people are away.
  • Communion roles only on communion Sundays, set for each service.
  • Requests arrive in Messages to accept or decline, and coordinators can send a gentle reminder.

Groups and messages

  • Care Groups, Midweek Groups and leadership together under Groups, with when and where each one meets.
  • Chat-style messages inside the app, with ticks that show when a message has arrived and been read.
  • Anyone can message the Office or a minister from inside the app, without hunting for a number.

Staff, records and library

  • Staff leave built in: allowances, approvals, public holidays and church closures.
  • Protected archives for minutes, plans and contracts, opened only by the roles you choose.
  • A church library with loans, returns and reminders.

Made for churches with little money and a weak signal.

Many churches pay for every megabyte and have no IT person. Church Hub was written with them in mind.

Works with no signal

No signal in the church hall? Church Hub still opens and you can look things up. Changes wait until you're back online.

Easy on data

An update downloads only the parts that changed, not the whole app again. It runs in the browser on any phone, with an Android app too.

Your people stay yours

Each church keeps its records in its own Google account, not ours. Everyone sees only what their role needs, with privacy pages for your country's law, from POPIA to GDPR.

Questions churches ask

Do we need someone technical to set it up?

No. Church Hub is designed to be set up and run by a church office volunteer. Each church's records live in its own Google account, so you need a Google account for the church.

Who can see our members' details?

Only the people whose role at church needs them. Members choose what others may see, and appear in the directory only if they agree. Sensitive details, like pastoral notes and children's medical notes, are kept to the people who care for them, with a record of who has looked.

Many of our couples share one email address. Is that a problem?

No. Up to four people can share one email address, and each still gets their own page, rosters and messages.

Is the price per church or per person?

Per church. However many people, groups or services you have, it's one coffee a month, or ten a year.

How do we pay?

By bank transfer (EFT) from your own banking app. Church Hub shows you its bank details and your church's own short reference, so we know the payment is from you. From outside South Africa, your bank changes your money into rand. Card payments are planned.

What if we can't pay one month?

Take a payment holiday: a free month, no questions asked. Monthly churches can take three a year, and yearly churches one. If paying is hard for longer, pray instead: Church Hub stays free for as long as you need. Nothing is ever locked or deleted for being late.

Is our prayer card safe to share?

A prayer card only holds what your church chooses: a photo, your town and two prayer points. Churches in countries where Christians face danger start hidden, shown only as “a church in” a wide region. Pray for things, not people by name: Church Hub flags phone numbers, email addresses and links before a card is saved.

When can our church sign up?

Church Hub is in daily use at a parish church in South Africa and is getting ready for other churches. The prayer circle starts when it opens to them, and any promise you make before then is kept. Try the demo now and see if it's right for you.
