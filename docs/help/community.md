# Community board and Marketplace

Two parts for a church family that helps each other. A **keeper** checks every notice and listing before anyone else sees
it, and people reply privately in **Messages**, never in public threads. An Administrator switches them on under
**Settings → Apps**. The first keepers are the Administrators, the Pastors and the church secretary; the Administrator can
choose others.

## How do I put a notice on the Community board?
For: Everyone
Also asked: I need a plumber. I can offer a lift. Say thank you. Pin a notice.

Open **Community board** and tap **Pin a notice**. Choose **I need help**, **I can offer**, **Notice** or **Thank you**, give
it a short title and say what you need. A keeper checks it first ("Pinned! A keeper will check it soon."). When it's up it stays for
30 days; you'll be asked **Renew for 30 days** before it ends, and **Sorted** takes it down when you're done. The latest three
notices also show on Home and on your Me page. Prayer requests and pastoral needs belong with the ministers – **write to the Office**.

## Someone replied to my notice. Where is it?
For: Everyone

Tap **I can help** on a notice to write to its owner. It goes privately to them in **Messages**. If a keeper asks you to change
a notice, or doesn't put it up, they tell you why in Messages.

## How do I sell, buy or give something away?
For: Church Partners
Also asked: Marketplace. For sale. List something.

The **Marketplace** is for Church Partners only. Open it and tap **List something**: give it a title, a price (or tick
**Open to offers**), the condition, your suburb only (never your address) and up to 3 photos, which are made small to save data.
Everyone who lists something first accepts the **Marketplace rules**. A keeper checks the listing before it shows; you're told
when it's up (30 days). To buy, tap **I'm interested** – it goes to the seller privately in Messages. Use **Mark as sold**
or **Mark as given away** when it's gone. The church is not part of any sale; meet at church after a service, and don't pay before you've seen it.

## How do keepers check notices and listings?
For: Keepers, Administrators
Also asked: Approve a notice. Ask for a change. Take it down.

Waiting items show at the top ("Notices waiting for a keeper"). For each one you can put it up, **Ask for a change** (with a kind
word on why), or not put it up, with a note – the person is told in Messages. If a member **Reports to the keepers**, the notice shows as reported and
you can **Take it down**. **Keepers…** on the Community board lets the Administrator add or change keepers.
