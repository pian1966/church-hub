# Finding your way around

## Where do I find help? How do I ask a question?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Help page. I'm stuck. Ask Church & Trellis a question. How do I use this? Who can I ask? Can I say my question?

Open **Help** in the menu (on a phone: **More** → **Help**). Type your question – or tap the microphone and say it – and tap
**Ask**. If your church has switched on AI, you get a short answer written for you, in your language, from these help pages,
with the pages it came from underneath so you can check. If not, you see the help pages that match your question. You can
also tap a topic under **Browse by topic** to read its questions.

If Help can't find your answer, **write to the Office** (**Messages**) and someone at church will help.

## Is what I ask Help kept or private?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Does Help save my questions? Who sees what I type? Is Help safe? Does the AI know my details?

Nothing you type or say into Help is saved, logged or shared – the question and the answer stay on your screen only. If your
church has switched on AI, your question (up to 300 characters) goes to Google's Gemini with the help pages that match it, the
kind of access you have and your language – never your records, your contact details or the church's lists. Because the
question goes as you wrote it, **please don't type private matters or other people's names** into Help. For anything personal,
use **Messages** to write to the Office or a pastor.

## Help gave me a wrong answer, or couldn't find one. What now?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Help doesn't understand me. The answer is out of date. It says I can't do something I can. No answer. Try again.

1. Ask again in other words – shorter is better: **How do I print the roster?** works better than a long story. You can also tap the microphone and say it.
2. Under the answer, open the help pages it used (they are listed) – the whole page often has the step you need.
3. Tap a topic under **Browse by topic** and read its questions.
4. Check the screen itself: the buttons are named in **bold** in the answers, exactly as they appear. If a button has a different name, your church may be using its own words, or Church & Trellis was updated – see **How do I update the app?**
5. Still stuck? **Write to the Office** (**Messages**). If an answer was wrong, tell them so it can be put right.

## How do I sign in?
For: Everyone
Also asked: How do I log in? What's my password? How do I get into Church & Trellis?

Church & Trellis has no password. Use the email address the church office has for you:
- **Sign in with Google** – if your email is a Gmail or Google address, tap it and choose your account.
- **Email me a link** – type your email address and tap **Email me a link**. Open the email and tap the link, and you're signed in.

- **Sign in with my number** – no email? Sign in with your cellphone number (or church number) and your 6-digit PIN. The
  first time, you need a **sign-in code** from the church office: tap **I have a sign-in code** and choose your PIN.

You can only sign in if the church office has given you access. If you see **No access yet**, ask the church office.

## I don't have an email address. How do I sign in?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Sign-in code. No email. Code from the office. The code doesn't work. PIN. Forgot my PIN. Church number. New phone.

**The first time:** ask the church office for a **sign-in code**. Scan its QR code with your phone's camera (or tap the link
they sent by WhatsApp), or open Church & Trellis, tap **I have a sign-in code** and type the 8 letters and numbers. Check the name
is yours and tap **Yes, that's me**. Then check your cellphone number and choose a **6-digit PIN** (not an easy one like
123456), typed twice. No number of your own? Tap **I don't have my own number**: Church & Trellis gives you a **church number** –
write it down. A code works **once** and for **14 days**; "Already been used" or "expired"? Ask the office for a new one.

**After that:** tap **Sign in with my number** and type your cellphone number (or church number) and your PIN – on any phone,
also a new one. Signing out doesn't change anything. **Forgot your PIN** or **changed your number**? Ask the office for a new
sign-in code and choose a new PIN; the old one stops working.

## I asked for a sign-in link but nothing came. What now?
For: Everyone
Also asked: The email link didn't arrive. The link doesn't work.

- Check the spam or junk folder, and that you typed the same email address the church office has for you.
- Open the link on the same phone or computer where you asked for it. If you open it somewhere else, Church & Trellis asks you to
  **Confirm your email** – type the same address again.
- Links can only be used once and expire after a while. If it doesn't work, ask for a new one.

## How do I sign in in the Android app, or on a home-screen icon?
For: Everyone
Also asked: The link opens in my browser instead of the app.

Email links can't open inside the app. In the app tap **Email me a link** (or **I already have a sign-in link** if you
already have one). Then open the email, **press and hold the sign-in link and choose Copy link**, come back to Church & Trellis,
paste it in the box and tap **Sign in**. If the church has set it up, **Sign in with Google** works in the Android app too.

## My spouse and I share one email address. How does that work?
For: Everyone
Also asked: We use the same email. How do I switch to my husband / wife? Who is using Church & Trellis?

People who share an email address (up to four, often a couple) share one sign-in. After signing in, Church & Trellis asks
**Who is using Church & Trellis?** – tap your name. Each person sees their own details, rosters and role. To change to the
other person, tap **Switch person** (the people icon at the top; on a phone, under **More**). The device remembers who
was using it until you sign out.

## Is there a phone app?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Can I install Church & Trellis? Is it on the Play Store / App Store?

- **Android**: there is a Church & Trellis app. Tap **Get the app** on Home (or the link on the Me page, **Get the Church & Trellis app for Android**,
  or under **About**). It isn't in the Play Store, so your phone may ask you to allow installing it.
- **iPhone**: open Church & Trellis in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**. Church & Trellis shows how
  (**Church & Trellis on your iPhone**).
- On any computer or phone you can also just use the website in a browser.

## How do I update the app?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Am I on the latest version? Something looks old.

Open **About** and tap **Check for updates**. If there's a newer version, tap **Update now**. In the Android app it also
tells you when a newer app is available – tap **Download the new app**. Church & Trellis also checks once each time About opens.

## Can I use Church & Trellis without the internet?
For: Everyone
Also asked: It says I'm offline. Why can't I change anything?

Yes, to look at things. Church & Trellis keeps a copy on your device, so you can open it and read your rosters, details and
so on without a signal. While you're offline it is **view-only**: you can't make changes until you're back online. When
the connection returns it says **Back online – you can make changes again.**

## How do I change the language?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Can I use Church & Trellis in Afrikaans / isiZulu / isiXhosa / another language?

Use the **globe** drop-down: on a computer at the bottom of the menu on the left, on a phone under **More**, and on the
sign-in screen. UK English, Afrikaans, isiZulu and isiXhosa are built in, with 19 more languages to choose from (including
US English). The choice
is kept on that device. Some screens for staff stay in English for now. See also [translations.md](translations.md).

## How do I switch to dark mode?
For: Everyone
Also asked: The screen is too bright. Light mode.

Use the **Appearance** drop-down (the half-shaded circle) next to the language: **Automatic** (same as this device),
**Light** or **Dark**. It's kept on that device.

## How do I add or change my photo?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Change my profile picture.

In the Android app or a home-screen icon, Church & Trellis suggests **Add a photo of yourself** if you don't have one – tap
**Take or choose a photo** (or **Not now** to be asked again in 30 days). On your own page you can also use **Add photo** /
**Change photo**. Drag and zoom until your face fills the circle (**Choose the face**), then **Use this photo**. Photos
must be JPG or PNG. Staff can add or change photos on a person's page.

## How do I sign out?
For: Everyone

On a computer, **Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu; on a phone, tap **More**, then **Sign out**. You'll need to sign
in again next time. Signing out also removes the copy kept on that device – do this on a shared computer.

## What's on the Home page?
For: Everyone
Also asked: What is the to-do list? What's new? Did you know? Coming up. Why does Home look different from someone else's?

Home is the first page you see, and it is different for each person, because it shows what concerns you:
- **To do** – things that need you, such as roster turns to reply to, unread messages, or (for staff) requests waiting for an
  answer. **Nothing needs you right now.** means you're up to date.
- **A birthday or anniversary card** – on your own birthday, or wedding anniversary, a warm card with a few falling petals greets you here and on your **Me** page.
- **Church health** – for Administrators only: a short list of things in the church's records worth a look, each marked **Weekly**,
  **Every 2 weeks** or **Monthly** for how often it is worth looking. Tap a line to go and put it right. It shows counts, not
  names. **All clear** means nothing needs a look.
- **Coming up** – events at church you can sign up for (**Events**).
- **What's new** – a short line for each change in Church & Trellis that you would notice. You only see the ones that concern
  you, so an Administrator and a member see different lists.
- **Did you know?** – a tip about something useful you may not have found yet, chosen for what you can do. It changes each time you sign in.
- Your **apps** as cards (for example **Rosters**, **Messages**, **Library**), and – if your church has it – the **prayer card** of
  another church to pray for this week and the latest notices from the **Community board**.

If something you expect isn't on Home, the part of Church & Trellis may be switched off, or your access doesn't include it –
see **Why can't I see a page or button that someone else can?** below.

## What is the 🎂 or 💍 beside someone's name?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Birthday today. Wedding anniversary today. Cake icon. Ring icon. Happy birthday. Anniversary card. Why didn't I get a birthday card? Who can see my birthday?

On someone's birthday a small **🎂 Birthday today** shows beside their name, and on a wedding anniversary a **💍 Wedding anniversary today**.
Church Partners see it in the church directory (**People**) for everyone who agreed to be in the directory; Pastors and
Administrators see it for everyone; carers and Midweek Group leaders see 🎂 on their group's cards when the person agreed to share
with their group. Only the day is shared – never the year or anyone's age. On your own birthday and anniversary you get a card on **Home** and **Me**.

Church & Trellis never celebrates a wedding anniversary for someone who is widowed, divorced, separated or single, or whose
spouse has died, and never for someone who has died or left the church. If you didn't get a card, check **Me → My details**:
your birthday, marital status and **Wedding anniversary** must be filled in – ask the church office to add them.

## How do I stop my birthday being shown?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Hide my birthday. Don't celebrate my birthday. I don't want people to know it's my birthday. Turn off the cake.

Open **Me → My details** and, under **Birthdays and anniversaries**, untick **Celebrate my birthday and anniversary**. Nobody sees
🎂 or 💍 beside your name any more, your birthday is taken out of the church directory, and you get no card. Tick it again to turn it back on.
To be taken out of the church directory altogether, change your **Church directory** choice under **Me → Consent**.

## What is "Birthdays this week" on Home?
For: Pastors, Administrators, Carers, Group leaders
Also asked: Upcoming birthdays. Who has a birthday soon? Birthday list. Anniversaries this week. May be a hard day.

Home shows the birthdays and wedding anniversaries of the next seven days, soonest first. Pastors and Administrators see everyone;
carers and Midweek Group leaders see the people in the groups they lead who agreed to share their birthday with the group. On the day,
Pastors and Administrators can tap **Send a message**. People who turned celebrations off are not listed.

Pastors also see **May be a hard day**: someone whose husband or wife has died, on the anniversary of the death or of their
wedding – a call or a visit may mean a lot. Only Pastors see it, and each time it is shown it is written in the audit log.

## Why can't I see a page or button that someone else can?
For: Everyone
Also asked: A menu item is missing. I can't find Rosters. Why is it greyed out? Where did the Library go? No access. Not allowed.

Church & Trellis shows each person only what their kind of access allows, so two people can see different menus. The usual reasons:
1. **Your kind of access doesn't include it.** For example, only Administrators see **Settings** and **Import**, and only Pastors see
   pastoral notes. Ask the church office if you think you should have it – an Administrator can change it under
   **Settings → Access and roles**.
2. **You haven't been given a job in that part yet.** Some things belong to a job: carers, Midweek Group leaders, **keepers**
   (who check Community board notices and Marketplace listings), event organisers and librarians.
3. **You aren't a Church Partner yet.** People who come to church but haven't become Church Partners see less, and the church
   decides how much. You can apply under **Become a Church Partner** on your **Me** page.
4. **The part is switched off.** An Administrator can switch parts on or off under **Settings → Apps**.
5. **On a phone**, only the main parts are in the tabs at the bottom – tap **More** for the rest.

If none of these explains it, **write to the Office** (**Messages**).

## What do the words mean? (Church Partner, attendee, keeper…)
For: Everyone
Also asked: Glossary. What is a Church Partner? What is an attendee? What is a keeper? What is a Roster coordinator? What is Basic access? What is a carer?

- **Administrator** – looks after Church & Trellis for the church: settings, access, privacy and imports.
- **Pastor** – sees people, households and pastoral notes. The **Senior Pastor** (the Rector, if your church uses that word) can also
  choose what the other pastors may do.
- **Roster coordinator** – makes the rosters and looks after the teams.
- **Children's ministry** – sees children, their guardians and medical notes.
- **Basic access** – everyone else who signs in: you see your own details, serving and leave.
- **Church Partner** – an adult who has agreed to be in the church directory. Church Partners see the church directory and can use more of Church & Trellis.
- **Attendee** – someone who comes to church but isn't a Church Partner yet. The church decides what attendees may do (**Settings → Attendees**).
- **Keeper** – a person who checks every notice on the **Community board** and every listing on the **Marketplace** before it shows.
- **Carer** – looks after a **Care Group**. **Midweek Group leader** – leads a Bible study or life group.
- **Event organiser** – may put up events and see who signed up.

Your church may use its own words (for example **Parishioner** for Church Partner, or **Rector** for Pastor) – Help uses
the words your church chose when it answers you.

## Can I try Church & Trellis without real data?
For: Everyone

Yes – the demo has made-up people: choose **Try the demo with made-up people** on the sign-in screen, then choose who to
be (each role sees different things). Changes stay in your browser; **Reset demo** starts again.
