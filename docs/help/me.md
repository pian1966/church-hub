# The Me page – your details, days away and serving

**Me** is everyone's own page: **My details**, **Days away** and **Serving**. On a phone it is the first tab for members.

## How do I change my phone number, email or address?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Update my details. I've moved. I have a new cell number. My name is spelt wrong.

Open **Me → My details** and tap **Change my details**. Change what's wrong and save. You can correct your name and
contact details yourself. Some things (shown under **Kept by the church office**) can only be changed by the church
office – ask them.

## I got married / had a baby. How do I tell the church?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Change my marital status. Add my spouse. Add my children. Wedding anniversary.

On **Me → My details**, use **Ask to change these** for your family details: marital status, wedding anniversary, your
spouse or partner (their full name) and **Your children** (one per line, with their birthday, e.g. *Sam Jacobs, 3 May
2015*). Tap **Send to the church office**. Nothing changes until the church office checks it; until then it says
**Waiting for the church office to check**, and you can **Withdraw** it. You can have one request at a time.

## How do I change what I agreed to (consent)?
For: Everyone
Also asked: I don't want to be in the church directory. Stop sending me WhatsApps. Privacy choices.

Open **Me → My details**, go to **Consent** and tap **Change my choices**. You can give or withdraw each one – for
example being in the church directory, sharing your details with your care group, and what you'd like to receive (weekly
notices, prayer needs, missionary updates). Withdrawing takes effect straight away. See also [privacy.md](privacy.md).

## How do I tell the church I'm away / can't serve on certain days?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Block out dates. I'm on holiday. Unavailable dates. Mark myself unavailable.

Open **Me → Days away** and tap **Add days away**. Choose the days (one or more stretches) and, if you like, a reason
(**Only the roster team sees it**). You won't be put on the roster on those days. To take them off, use **Remove away
dates**. If you're already on the roster on a day you're away, Church Hub warns you – tap **Can't make it** for that turn.
Staff: approved leave is added here automatically (**From Leave**).

## Where do I see when I'm serving?
For: Everyone
Also asked: When am I on duty? My roster. What's my next turn?

Open **Me → Serving**. **Coming up** lists your turns on the roster. Reply to each with **Accept** or **Can't make it**,
so the roster coordinator knows. **Where I serve** shows your teams and positions. If you're not on the roster soon, it
says **You're not on the roster in the coming weeks.**

## I can't make a turn I accepted. What do I do?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Swap my turn. Cancel my duty. I'm sick on Sunday.

On **Me → Serving**, find the turn and tap **Can't make it**. The roster coordinator sees it and can find someone else.
Church Hub doesn't arrange swaps between people yet – if you've found someone to swap with, tell the roster coordinator.

## How do I offer to help in a new area?
For: Everyone
Also asked: I'd like to join the welcome team. How do I volunteer? Join a team.

On **Me → Serving**, under where you'd like to help, choose the position and tap **Offer to help** (**I'd like to help**).
Say how often you could serve (every week, every 2 weeks, once a month or now and then), which services, and anything
the roster coordinator should know, then **Send to the roster coordinator**. It shows **Waiting for the roster
coordinator** until they answer; you can **Withdraw** it. If they reply **Not now**, you'll see their reply under the
position until you tap OK.

## How do I serve more or less often, or stop serving somewhere?
For: Everyone
Also asked: I'm too busy, can I serve less? Leave a team. Change services.

On **Me → Serving**, find the position and tap **Change how you serve**. Choose a new frequency or services, or **I'd like
to stop serving here**, add a note (e.g. *busy with exams until June*), and send it to the roster coordinator.

## Can I serve with my family or friends?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Put me on the same Sunday as my wife. Keep us together.

Yes. On **Me → Serving**, use **Serve with family or friends** and say who you'd like to serve with. The roster will try
to keep you together. (Roster coordinators can also set people to be kept apart – e.g. parents taking turns with a baby.)

## Can I download the Android app from here?
For: Everyone

Yes – **Get the Church Hub app for Android** is on the Me page. See [getting-started.md](getting-started.md).

## Where is my care group / life group?
For: Members

If you're in one, **My care group** and **My life groups** show on the Me page. See [care-groups.md](care-groups.md)
and [life-groups.md](life-groups.md).
