# Languages and better wording

## Which languages can I use?
For: Everyone

UK English, Afrikaans, isiZulu and isiXhosa are built in, and 19 more can be chosen (Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga,
siSwati, Tshivenḓa, isiNdebele, chiShona, Kiswahili, Amharic, Yorùbá, French, Portuguese, Spanish, German, Dutch, Italian,
Polish, American English). Choose with the **globe** drop-down – see [getting-started.md](getting-started.md). The translations were drafted
by AI, so some may read oddly; staff-only screens and the church's own words stay in English for now.

## The wording in my language is wrong. How do I fix it?
For: Everyone using a language other than English
Also asked: Bad translation. Suggest a better word.

At the foot of every page, tap **Help improve the … wording on this page**. Find the text (**On the page you were on**, or
**All texts**), write **Your wording**, add a note if useful, and **Send suggestion**. Someone who checks that language will
look at it; you see your suggestions under **Your suggestions** and can **Take back this suggestion**. Keep any bold marks and
{name}-style parts exactly as in the English – Church Hub puts a name, number or date there.

## How do I check suggestions for a language?
For: Language checkers, Administrators

Open **Translations → To check**. For each suggestion: **Use this**, **Change it first**, or **Not this time**. Approved
wording shows for everyone at once (after signing in). **Go back to the original** undoes it.

## How do I choose who checks a language?
For: Administrators

**Translations → Who checks** – choose a person for each language. Until someone is chosen, suggestions come to the
Administrators. **Download the changed wording** gives a file the developer can build into Church Hub.
