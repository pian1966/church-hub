# Midweek groups (Bible studies, life groups, Connect Groups)

Midweek groups work like care groups, with a **leader** instead of a carer. Each group's leader names it (e.g. *Tuesday
Bible study*). The church switches them on in Settings → Apps (they're off until then), and may call them something else
under Church Words (e.g. Connect Groups or Life groups). You can be in several.

## Which midweek group am I in?
For: Members
Also asked: My Bible study. My small group. My life group. My Connect Group.

Open **Midweek groups** or your **Me** page (**My midweek groups**). If you're not in one, ask the church office or a group
leader to add you.

## How do I join a midweek group?
For: Members
Also asked: Find a group near me. Ask to join a Bible study.

Ask the church office or the group's leader to add you. A **Find a group** page and **Ask to join** are planned but not
built yet, and so are meeting days, times and a map of where groups meet.

## What can my midweek group see about me?
For: Members

Your name, and – if you are in the church directory (you agreed to this when you became a Church Partner) – your photo,
phone, email, address and birthday. Change your choices under **Me → My details → Consent**.

## How do I rename my group?
For: Group leaders

On **Midweek groups**, open your group and tap **Rename** – give it the name your group uses (e.g. *Tuesday Bible
study*). Leaders can't change who leads it.

## How do I add or remove people from my group?
For: Group leaders

Open your group: **Add someone to the group** (type part of their name – people in the church directory), or **Take out of
the group**.

## How do I message my group?
For: Group leaders

Tap **Send the group a message** on the Midweek groups page or your Me page. See [messages.md](messages.md).

## How do I set up midweek groups and leaders?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Turn on Bible studies. Add a life group. Make someone a group leader.

Switch on **Midweek groups** in **Settings → Apps**. Then on **Midweek groups** tap **Add a midweek group**, add people, and
tap the star next to someone to make them **Group leader**. You can also **Rename** or **Delete the group**.
