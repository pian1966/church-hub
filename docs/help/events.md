# Events

Events are the things the church puts on that people sign up for – a men's breakfast, a family day, a working bee. People
sign up with one tap and are reminded 3 days and 1 day before. The church switches Events on in **Settings → Apps** (it's off
until then).

## Where do I see what's on at church, and how do I sign up?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Upcoming events. I'll be there. Put my name down. Sign up for the breakfast. What's coming up?

Open **Events**. It shows what's coming up at church. Tap **I'll be there** on the event you'd like to come to – that's all.
Under **Events** you can also see **My events** (the ones you've signed up for) and **Past** ones. If the church has closed
sign-up for an event, it says **Sign-up for this event has closed**; some events say **No need to sign up – just come.**
Tap **Add to my calendar** to put the day in your phone's calendar.

## Will I be reminded? Can I get the reminder by email too?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Event reminder. Reminders by email. Why did I get an email about an event?

Yes. When you've signed up, Church & Trellis reminds you in **Messages** 3 days and 1 day before. If the church offers it, you
can also tick **Email me reminders too** and type your **Email address** when you sign up. That is only for that one event: your
email address is kept with your sign-up and deleted after the event. (Email reminders only work if your church has set up its
email robot – an Administrator can read how in Settings → Apps → Events.)

## Can I bring guests or my family?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Bring a friend. How many people can come with me? Guests counter.

If the event allows guests, **Bringing guests?** appears when you sign up. Use **One more** and **One less** to say how many
(it shows **Up to {n} guests**, which is what the organiser allowed). Family members who don't have their own sign-in come as
guests, so allow a few.

## I can't come any more. What do I do?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Cancel my sign-up. Take my name off. Un-sign up.

Open the event in **Events** and tap **I can't come after all**, then **Yes, I can't come**. Someone else can then have your
place. If you signed up guests, they come off the list too.

## How do I put up an event?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Event organisers
Also asked: Create an event. New event. Add a function to the calendar.

Open **Events** and tap **New event**. Fill in the title (for example *Men's breakfast*), the date and time, and the place (for an
event away from church, add a map link). Then choose:
- **What it's about** and **Who it's for**.
- **People sign up** – they tap **I'll be there** and you see who's coming. Leave **Sign-up closes** empty to keep it open until the day.
- **Guests each may bring** – how many guests one person may sign up.
- **Offer reminders by email too** – people who ask also get the 3-day and 1-day reminders by email (needs the church's email robot; **Settings → Apps → Events**).

Tap **Put it up**. People see it under **Events** straight away.

## Who can put up events? How do I choose the organisers?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Event organisers. Who can create events. Add an organiser.

Organisers put events up, see who's coming and write to them. Until you choose, they are the Administrators, the Pastors and the
church secretary. On **Events**, open **Event organisers…**, type part of a person's name under **Add an organiser**, and choose
them. **Use the first organisers again** goes back to the original three.

## How do I see who's coming, and write to them?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Event organisers
Also asked: Who's coming? Attendance list. Message everyone coming.

Open the event and look under **Who's coming**. Tap **More → Message everyone coming** to write to them all in **Messages**. **Print the list**
or **Download the list** if you need it on paper. To take someone off, use **Take off the list** beside their name (**Take all of
them off the list** clears it).

## I need a list of everyone who signed up, with their phone numbers.
For: Administrators, Pastors, Event organisers, Roster coordinators, Children's ministry
Also asked: Sign-up report. Print the sign-up sheet. Save as PDF.

On **Events**, open **More → Sign-up report…**, choose **Which events**, then **Print** – or choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to make a
PDF. Tick **Also show email addresses** if you need them, or tap **Download (spreadsheet)**. It has people's personal details on
it, so keep it safe and shred it after the event.

## Someone put their name down on paper. How do I add them?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Event organisers, Roster coordinators, Children's ministry
Also asked: Add people to an event. Sign-up sheet. They told me in person. Add someone who can't use the app.

Open the event and tap **Add people**. Type part of the person's name under **Find someone**, then tap **Add to the list**. It's in
their name, so they see it under **Events** and get the reminders. If they aren't in People yet, tap **Not in People yet? Add them**:
they're added to **People** as an Attender, in a household of their own, and put on the list (add more details later).

## I have a whole paper sign-up sheet. Can Church & Trellis read it?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Event organisers, Roster coordinators, Children's ministry
Also asked: From a sheet. Photo of the sign-up list. Scan names. Read handwriting.

Yes. Open the event, tap **From a sheet** and take a photo (or choose one – from Google Photos or your gallery). Church & Trellis reads
the names, finds each person in **People**, and shows them for you to check. **You check every name before anyone is added.**
A "+2" beside a name becomes that person's guests. Reading photos needs AI: an Administrator switches it on in **Settings →
Apps**; without it you can type the names instead. The photo goes to Google's Gemini AI to read the handwriting, and Church &
Trellis doesn't keep it. If the photo is in Google Photos, open it there, tap **Share → Save to device**, then choose it here.

## How do I cancel an event?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Event organisers
Also asked: The event is off. Rain. Called off.

Open the event, tap **More → Cancel the event…**, write a short message, then **Cancel it and tell them**. Everyone coming is told in **Messages**.
The event stays on the list, marked **Cancelled**, so people who look for it know. If it's back on, tap **It's on again**. To remove an
event for good (and its sign-ups), tap **More → Delete**, then **Yes, delete**.
