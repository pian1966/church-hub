# Community board

The Community board is a corkboard of sticky notes: ask for help, offer it, share a notice or say thank you. A keeper checks
every notice before anyone else sees it. The church switches it on in **Settings → Apps** (it's off until then). Church Partners,
staff and leaders can see and use it; Attenders (people who only come along) don't.

## How do I put a notice on the board?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Post a notice. Ask for a plumber. Offer a lift. Lost umbrella. Pin a note. Say thank you.

Open **Community board** and tap **Pin a notice**. Choose **What kind of notice?** – **I need help**, **I can offer**, **Notice** or
**Thank you** – and give it a title (for example *I need a plumber, or a lift to church from Hilton*). Add **A few words more**
if you like. Add a **Phone number to show** only if you want people to phone you; otherwise they write to you in **Messages**.
Tap **Send to a keeper**. It shows **Waiting for a keeper** until a keeper puts it up, and you get a message when it's up. Prayer
requests and pastoral needs belong with the ministers – write to the Office instead.

## How long does a notice stay up? How do I renew or take it down?
For: Everyone
Also asked: My notices. Renew. Remove my notice. It's sorted now.

A notice stays up for 30 days. In its last few days you're asked to renew it – tap **Renew for 30 days** (up to twice). Your own notices are under
**My notices**. When you've been helped, tap **Sorted**: it stays on the board with a "Sorted" stamp for a day, then goes. If a
keeper **asked you to change** a notice, you get a message with their note – open it, change the notice, and send it again.

## Someone's notice worries me. What do I do?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Report a notice. Inappropriate post.

Tap **Report to the keepers** on the notice. The keepers will have a look. The church doesn't vouch for the people or offers on
the board – please arrange things between yourselves, wisely and kindly.

## I'm a keeper. How do I check notices?
For: Administrators, Pastors, Keepers
Also asked: Approve a notice. Waiting for a keeper. Ask for a change. Take it down.

On **Community board**, notices **waiting for a keeper** are listed at the top. Look at each, then tap **Approve** to put it up,
**Ask for a change** (write **What should they change?** – it goes to the person in **Messages**), or **Decline** (write a kind word
on why). On a notice already up, **Take it down** removes it, **Message {name}** writes to the person privately, and reported
notices show **Reported by {n} people**.

## Who are the keepers? How do I change them?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Add a keeper. Who approves notices. Keepers for the board and the Marketplace.

Keepers check every new notice before anyone else sees it. Until you choose, they are the Administrators, the Pastors and the
church secretary. On **Community board**, open **More → Keepers…**, type part of a name under **Add a keeper**, and choose them.
**Use the first keepers again** goes back to the original three. Under **More → The note on the board…** you can write the note
shown at the foot of the board in your own words. The Marketplace has its own keepers, chosen the same way.

## Can I hide the board's latest notices from my Home page?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Too many notices on Home. Remove the card.

Yes. Home shows **Latest on the Community board** (the newest three). Tap **Hide this from my Home**; the board is still in the
menu, and **See the board** opens it.
