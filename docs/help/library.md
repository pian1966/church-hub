# Library (the church's books, DVDs and more)

The Library lists the books, DVDs, CDs, audiobooks, magazines and games the church owns and shows whether each is in.
Librarians add them and write down who borrows and returns them. The church switches it on in Settings → Apps (it's off
until then).

## Where is the library's policy?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Library rules. How long can I keep a book?

Open **Library**: the policy is at the top, under **Library policy**. Tap **Read the policy** to read it as a page. If it is
a file, tap **Open** (a PDF or picture) or **Download**.

## How do I add or change the library's policy?
For: Librarians, the Senior Pastor

On **Library**, tap **Add the policy** (or **Edit** under **Library policy**). Paste the words in, or tap **Choose a file**
to attach a PDF, a Word document or a picture (at most 600 KB). A plain text file is put straight into the box. Everyone
who signs in can read it.

## Which books does the church have?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Is a book available? Find a book. Books by an author.

Open **Library** to browse the church's books, DVDs and other media. Use the filters for media type, title, author and
subject, and choose a field to sort by. Each item shows **Available** or **On loan**, and its shelf if the church filled
that in.

## How do I borrow a book?
For: Everyone

Ask a librarian – they lend it to you in Church Hub and it then appears under **My books** with the date it is due back.
Asking for a book inside Church Hub is planned but not built yet.

## Which books do I have, and when are they due?
For: Everyone

Open **Library** and look under **My books**. If a book is overdue, Home reminds you.

## Who can see what I have borrowed?
For: Everyone

Only the librarians and you. Everyone else sees only that the book is on loan. A loan is removed a year after the book
came back.

## How do I add a book, DVD or CD?
For: Librarians
Also asked: A book was donated. We bought new books. Record where a book came from. Add a DVD. Photograph a cover.

On **Library**, tap **Add to the library**. Choose **What it is** (a book, DVD, CD, audiobook, magazine, game or other); only
the title is needed. For the cover, tap **Take a photo** to use the phone's camera, or **Choose a picture**. You can also fill
in the author, a category, how many copies there are, how it was acquired (bought or donated), from whom, the date, and its
shelf.

## How do I lend a book and take it back?
For: Librarians
Also asked: Check out a book. Return a book. Mark a book as returned.

Tap **Lend** next to the item, check the **Date borrowed** date (three weeks unless you change it), type part of the borrower's
name and pick them. Every borrower needs a mobile number so an item can be traced: if their record has none, Church Hub asks
you to type one. For **someone not on the church's list**, open that part and type their name and mobile number.
When the item comes back, tap **Returned** next to it under **On loan**. You can add a note about damage or anything else;
the borrower's mobile number is then cleared. Overdue items are marked, and Home tells you how many there are.

## How do I import a list of books?
For: Librarians
Also asked: Upload books from a spreadsheet. Bring in a book list. CSV.

Save the list from Excel or Google Sheets as **CSV**. The first row must hold the headings, one of them **Title**; any of
**Author**, **Type** (book, DVD, CD…), **Category**, **Copies**, **Shelf**, **ISBN**, **Date acquired**, **How acquired**
(bought or donated), **Donated by** and **Note** are used too. On **Library**, tap **Import a list** and choose the file.
Church Hub shows how many items it will add, and leaves out rows without a title and items already in the library (the same
ISBN, or the same title and author). Tap **Import**. You can change any item afterwards with the pencil.

## What if an item is lost, damaged or given away?
For: Librarians
Also asked: Remove a book. Delete a book. A borrower lost a book.

If a borrower can't bring an item back, tap **Lost** next to it under **On loan** (tap again to be sure). The borrower's
mobile number is kept on that loan so it can be traced. To take something off the shelf for another reason, tap the bin next to
it, choose **Why** (lost, damaged, given away or other) and tap **Take out**. It stays in the reports and under **Taken out of
the library**, where **Put it back** returns it if it turns up. Only something added by mistake should be deleted completely
(under **Added by mistake?**).

Administrators and librarians can also permanently **Purge** a loan or a removed book from its activity list or report.
Church Hub asks you to confirm first. Purging a removed book also removes its linked loan records; the action is kept in the
audit log. It cannot be undone.

## How do I see what was lent, returned or lost?
For: Librarians
Also asked: Library report. Books acquired this year. Overdue list.

On **Library**, tap **Reports**, choose the **From** and **To** dates and tap **Show**. You see what was lent, returned,
lost, acquired and taken out in that time. **Download as a spreadsheet** saves it as a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets.

## Who are the librarians?
For: Administrators, the Senior Pastor

Administrators always are. An Administrator or the Senior Pastor chooses one or two more: open **Library → Librarians**, type
part of the person's name and pick them. They need to be able to sign in to Church Hub; the Library then shows them the
librarian's buttons. To choose someone else when there are two, take one out first.
