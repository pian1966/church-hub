Good to know

Your questions, answered

Who can see my details?

Only the people whose job at church needs them. Other members see you only if you agree to be in the church directory.

Can I change my mind?

Yes, any time. Your choices are on your own Me page.

No signal?

You can still look things up. Changes wait until you're back online.
