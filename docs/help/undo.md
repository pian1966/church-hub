# Undo and Version history

Made a mistake? Church & Trellis lets you take a change back straight away with **Undo**, and Administrators and Pastors can go back to
an earlier version of a person's record with **Version history** – much like Google Docs.

## How do I undo a mistake?
For: Everyone who changes records (Administrators, Pastors, Roster coordinators)
Also asked: Undo. I made a mistake. I changed the wrong person. I deleted something by accident. Take it back. Go back.

- Straight after you save, add or delete something, a small bar appears at the bottom of the screen with an **Undo** button.
- Tap **Undo** to put it back as it was. Tap it again to take back the change before that.
- Undo remembers your last 20 changes on this phone or computer, for 30 minutes. It only takes back **your own** changes made on
  **this** device.
- If someone else has changed the same record since, Church & Trellis says **Someone has changed this since. Undo anyway?** – tap
  **Undo** again only if you are sure.
- Undo works for people, households, groups, services, positions, teams, Leadership groups, events, library books and Midweek studies.

## How do I go back to an earlier version of someone's details?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: Version history. Restore an old version. See who changed someone's details. What did it say before. Go back in time.

- Open the person's page and tap **Version history**.
- Each change is listed, newest first: when it was made, who made it, and **What changed** (tap it to see the old and new details).
- **Go back to before this** puts the whole record back as it was before that change.
- **Put this back** (beside one detail) puts back only that detail and leaves everything else as it is now.
- Church & Trellis keeps each earlier version for 90 days. Medical notes, photos and ID numbers are never kept in the history.
- Every Undo and every version put back is written in the audit log (**Privacy → Audit log**).
