# Usage (for Administrators and Pastors)

**Usage** shows how many people are using Church & Trellis, who is on right now and what they use most. Only Administrators and
Pastors see it (**Usage** in the menu).

## What does the Usage page show?
For: Administrators, Pastors
Also asked: How many people are on the app. Who is using the app. Who hasn't signed in yet. Statistics.

- **Who is on the app** – the adults at the church (children are not counted): how many **Church Partners** and **Everyone else** are
  on the app, **Invited, not on yet** or **Not invited yet**.
- **On right now** – the people who opened Church & Trellis in the last 5 minutes.
- **How often it is used** – today, this week and this month, and how many people opened it each day for two weeks.
- **Growth** – how many people have started using the app, week by week, for 12 weeks.
- **Most used parts** – how often each part was opened in the last 30 days and by how many different people.
- **Which version people have**, **Busiest days** and **How people open it** (the Android app, added to the home screen, or a web browser).
- **How much people use it** – each adult at the church is placed on a five-step scale: **Not on the app yet**, **Just started**,
  **Getting going**, **Regular**, **Keen** and **Power user**, shown as five dots and a word. It comes from how many of the last 28 days
  the person opened the app and how many different parts they use (a part used by only one person doesn't make a Power user). Someone who was
  using the app but hasn't opened it for 3 weeks or more is marked **Slipping away** and listed under **Might need a nudge**. The same
  dots appear on a person's page, beside **Using Church & Trellis since**. Until Church & Trellis has recorded some usage, it shows only **On the app**.
- **Church Partners not on the app yet** – with an **Invite them** button that opens **People → Invitations**.

Every chart has **See the numbers** under it, which shows the same figures as a table.

## Who can see people's names on the Usage page?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Names appear only under **On right now**, **How much people use it** and **Church Partners not on the app yet**; the level on a person's page is shown only to Administrators and Pastors, never to the person themself. The counts for each part never say who used what.
Members, Roster coordinators and Children's ministry cannot open Usage.

## Why does Usage say "Nothing recorded yet"?
For: Administrators, Pastors

Who is on the app comes from the church's records, so it shows straight away. The rest (who is on now, the days, the versions and the
parts) builds up as people open Church & Trellis. In the demo, made-up usage is shown so you can see what the page looks like.
**Refresh** fetches the latest numbers.

## Does Church & Trellis keep track of when I use it?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Is the app watching me. Who can see when I was on. Privacy of my usage. Can the pastor see if I use the app.

Yes, a little, so the church can help people who haven't started and see which parts are worth improving. Church & Trellis notes
when you last opened it, which version you have, how you opened it (the Android app, from your home screen or in a web browser), and
for the last 35 days how many times you opened it and which parts – for example **Rosters** or **Messages**. It does **not** keep
what you type, search for or read, or who you message. Only Administrators and Pastors can see it, and older days are dropped after
35 days. It is deleted with the rest of your information if you ask the church to delete it.
