# Rosters

Everyone can reply to their roster turns (also on the Me page – see [me.md](me.md)). Roster coordinators and
Administrators plan the rosters. Rosters never put people in at random: families and friends who asked to serve together
are kept together, people who are away are skipped, turns are shared fairly, and every suggestion says why.

## How do I accept or decline a roster turn?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Confirm my duty. Say no to a roster. Reply to the roster.

When you're put on the roster you get a message in **Messages** – tap **Accept** or **Can't make it** right there. You can
also reply on **Me → Serving** (or **Rosters → My serving**). Turns waiting for your reply also show on your Home **To do**
list. If you say you can't make it, the roster team (Administrators, Roster coordinators and anyone the church chose, such
as the church secretary) gets a message so they can find someone else.

## How do I see the whole roster?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Who is on duty this Sunday? Show the roster.

Open **Rosters**. The table shows each position down the side, in the order of a service (Welcome, Service Leading,
Hospitality, Music and so on), and the services across the top in time order, each in its own colour. Use **‹ Earlier**
and **Later ›** to move between dates, or **Go to a date** to jump straight to a day. Colours show **Accepted**, **Waiting
for a reply**, and suggestions (dashed border). The top row (date, time, communion) stays in view as you scroll down.
Departments and positions that aren't needed at any of the services you're looking at (e.g. Creche at 8am, or communion
roles when there's no communion) are left out. Members see their own turns under **My serving**.

## How do I print or download the roster?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Print the roster. Roster as a PDF. Roster for the notice board. Save the roster. Portrait or landscape.

Roster coordinators: **Rosters → More → Print or download the roster**. Everyone else: **Rosters → My serving → Print or
download the roster**. Choose **how long** (1 month, 6 weeks, 2 months, a quarter, 6 months or a year, starting on the date
you pick – the next Sunday to begin with), the **page shape** (portrait or landscape) and **which services** (each regular
service gets its own page, in its own colours). The positions run down the side and every Sunday across the top. Tap
**Download PDF** to keep or send it, or **Print**. A Sunday on which a service isn't held stays in the table as a grey
column that says so, so nobody thinks it was forgotten. Positions nobody is on are left out, unless you tick **Also print
positions that are still empty**. Volunteers see the names of Church Partners who are serving – no contact details.
A service that was **cancelled** is marked the same way, as a narrow grey column with the note written up its side (and the
reason, if you gave one).

## How do I cancel a service?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Service cancelled. No service this Sunday. Public holiday. Put a service back. Tell volunteers a service is off. Don't delete the service.

Open **Rosters**, tap the **⋯** under the service's date and choose **Cancel this service…**. You can say why (for example
*Public holiday*), and if more than one service is held that day, cancel just this one or all of them. Tick **Let the people
serving know** to send each of them a friendly message in Church & Trellis – nothing is sent unless you tick it, and anyone
who can't open the app yet is counted so you can tell them yourself. The service stays on the roster as a narrow hatched
column marked **Cancelled** (and on the printed roster and PDF), nobody is asked for it, and it leaves "Please check" and
"Fill the gaps". Everyone who was on it stays on the roster, out of sight. To undo it, tap **⋯ → Put this service back…**:
the column is whole again, everyone is back, and you can tell them the good news. Volunteers see **Cancelled** on their
turn under Me → Serving. Don't delete the service from Settings to cancel it – that takes everyone off it.

## How many services can I see at once?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Show more Sundays. The roster is too wide. Hide the evening service.

On a computer or tablet the roster opens with as many services as fit your screen (**Fits your screen**). Next to
**Side by side:** use **−** and **+** to show from 1 to 16 services, tap **Fit my screen** to go back, or choose **1 month**
or **1 quarter**. When the roster is wider than the screen, a slider above it (and a scroll bar below) moves along. On a
phone you see one service at a time. To leave a service out for now, tap the
**eye** on its heading; it moves to **Hidden for now**, where one tap brings it back (**Show all** brings them all back).

## Before I make the roster, how do I find out who is away?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Ask who's away. Ask everyone for their days away. Term planning. Who's free?

On **Rosters**, open **More → Ask who's away…**. Choose the term (or type the **From** and **To** days) and tap **Ask them**. Everyone on a
team who uses Church & Trellis (and isn't taking a break) gets a message, **Are you away soon?**, and a card on **Me → Serving**. Each
person adds their days away or taps **I'm free – no days away**. **Rosters** then shows **Days away for {dates}** – how many have
answered, **{free} free, {away} away** – and **Remind the others** nudges those who haven't. People who can't sign in yet are counted, so
you can reach them by email, WhatsApp or SMS yourself. Their days away then count when you **fill the roster**.

## Someone can't make their turn. What happens?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Declined a turn. Cover for a turn. Someone asked to swap. Take my place.

When someone taps **Can't make it**, you get a message in **Messages**. They may first ask a teammate who is free that day to take their
place: if that person says **Yes, I'll do it**, the turn becomes theirs (already accepted) and the roster team is told, so there is
nothing for you to do. If they say **Sorry, I can't**, or nobody is asked, the turn shows as **Can't make it** and you can put
someone else on it (**Change person**).

## How do I fill in the roster?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Make the roster. Auto roster. Generate the roster.

1. Open **Rosters** and tap **Fill the gaps for me**.
2. Church & Trellis suggests people for the empty slots (dashed border). Hover or tap a suggestion to see why it chose them.
3. Tap **Keep these and ask people** – everyone suggested is asked to accept – or **Discard** to throw them away.

If some slots can't be filled, Church & Trellis lists them under **Why some slots are still empty** (for example, not enough
people on the team, or everyone is away). Add more people to the team, or ask someone directly.

## How do I put a particular person on the roster, or take them off?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Change who is serving. Remove someone from the roster. Mark someone as accepted.

On **Rosters**, tap the slot (the position on that date). Choose the person and **Add them**, or use **Take off the
roster**. To swap someone, tap their name and use **Change person**: pick someone else and the turn moves in one go. The
new person is asked, and the first person's "You're on the roster" message is taken back if they haven't opened it (or
they get a short "No longer needed" note). You can also **Mark accepted** or **Mark "can't make it"** for someone who told
you in person.

## Some people haven't replied. How do I remind them?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Chase up volunteers. Nobody answered.

On **Rosters**, open **More → Remind those who haven't replied**. Church & Trellis says how many people it will remind; tap
**Send reminders** and each of them gets a message in **Messages**.

## How do I set which Sundays have Holy Communion?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Communion servers every week. Only roster communion helpers when there's communion. Christmas communion.

Open **Rosters → Services**, choose the service and set which weeks of the month it has communion (every week, the 1st to
5th Sunday, the last Sunday, or never). Then tap the **Communion positions** – the ones only needed when there is
communion. Communion servers and providers are only asked for on those days. For a one-off change, such as Christmas or a
fifth Sunday, tap **Communion** under that date in the roster and choose **Communion this time** or **No communion this
time**.

## How do I set up teams and positions?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Add a department. Add a position. Create a new ministry team.

Open **Rosters → Teams**.
- First time: **Set up the church's departments** adds the church's departments and positions in one click.
- **New team** adds a department (e.g. *Prayer*); **Add a position** adds a position to it (e.g. *Camera*) in one box. Use
  **−** and **+** to set how many people it needs per service (e.g. 2 on the sound desk). To need a different number at one service
  (e.g. 2 at the 10am but 1 at the 5pm), tap that service (**8am**, **10am** or **5pm**) at the top first, then **−** or **+**: the number you
  set there applies to that service only, and the roster, the gaps and the printed roster follow it.
- Each position shows the services it is needed at (8am, 10am, 5pm) and how many people can serve any week, once a month or
  as a backup. Tap **8am**, **10am** or **5pm** to see only that service's teams. A position's names fold away until you tap them.
- A position added by mistake, or no longer used, can be removed with **⋯ → Delete position**.
- A position's **tooltip** (a short note about what the job involves) is written from the same **⋯** menu by the Administrator,
  the Rector and the assistant ministers. On a phone, tap the small **i** beside a position to read it.
- New suggested positions (such as Verger, Ushers or Recorded Music) are offered with **Add them**.
- Move a department up or down with the **↑ ↓** arrows; the roster shows them in that order.
- Two teams doing the same thing (e.g. Music and Worship)? On one of them, open **⋯ → Join with another team**: its
  positions, people and turns move across, and nobody is in it twice.
- A team with no positions can be removed with **⋯ → Delete team**.
- Teams can't be renamed yet.

## How do I add someone to a team?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Add a volunteer. Who can serve in each position?

Open **Rosters → Teams**, find the position and tap **Add team member**. Choose the person, how often they'd like to serve
and at which services. Staff can also do this from the person's page with **Edit volunteering**.

## Someone offered to serve. Where do I answer?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Volunteer requests. Someone wants to join the team. Offers to serve.

At the top of **Rosters → Teams** under **Offers to serve** (also on your Home **To do** list). Tap **Yes** to put them on
the team (or change how they serve), or **Not now** with a short reply they will see (e.g. *The team is full for now –
we'll ask you in the new year*).

## How do I keep people together, or apart?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators (members can ask to serve with someone)
Also asked: Married couple on the same Sunday. Parents shouldn't serve at the same time.

Open **Rosters → Serving together**.
- **Serve together**: choose at least two people and how important it is – **Always together** (rostered together or not
  at all, e.g. a family sharing a car) or **Together when possible**. "Together" means the same service, not always the same job.
- **Keep apart**: they will never be rostered at the same service – e.g. parents taking turns with a baby.

Members can tap **Ask to serve with someone** there, or use **Serve with family or friends** on the Me page.

## Can volunteers see who else is serving at their service?
For: Administrators, Roster coordinators
Also asked: Who's serving with you. Show or hide the crew. Turn off names on Me → Serving.

Yes. Under each of a volunteer's turns on **Me → Serving**, **Who's serving with you** shows the other people serving at that
service, with their short names and jobs (no contact details), only to people serving there. To switch it off, open
**Settings → Church Services** and untick **Volunteers see who else is serving at their services**.

## How do I see who is away?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Unavailable volunteers. Blockout dates.

Open **Rosters → Away dates**. It lists the dates people can't serve. Approved leave from the Leave app is included
automatically (**From Leave**). You can **Add away dates** for someone who told you in person.

## How do I change which positions run at each service?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: No creche at the 10am. Change a service's positions.

Open **Rosters → Services**, choose the service, and tap a position to switch it on or off at that service (**Switch all
on** / **Switch all off**). Its volunteers stay on the team. Administrators can also choose the service's **Look**
(colour and picture) and **Mood** (words volunteers see).

## How do I add a special service, like Good Friday or a funeral?
For: Roster coordinators, Administrators
Also asked: Christmas service. Add an extra service. Memorial service.

Open **Rosters → Services** and tap **Add a special service**. Choose from the suggestions (the dates of Easter-based
services are worked out for you) or **Add your own**. Then **Choose its positions** – each special service only has the
departments and positions you switch on for it.

## How do I add or remove a regular service time?
For: Administrators
Also asked: We're starting a 7pm service. Add a campus or venue. Stop the evening service.

Regular services are in **Settings → Services and venues** (add a time, day, campus or venue). Upcoming dated services are
listed under **Settings → Services that need a roster**, with a bin to remove one; **Stop holding a regular service…**
removes its services from a date. See [settings.md](settings.md).
