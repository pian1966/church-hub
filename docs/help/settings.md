# Settings and setup (for Administrators)

Only Administrators can change settings, unless they let Pastors (see *What Pastors can do*). Use **Search settings**
at the top of Settings to jump to one.

## How do I switch an app on or off?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Turn on midweek groups / Bible studies. Hide Leave. Switch off care groups.

**Settings → Apps**. Switching an app off hides it; nothing is deleted. Midweek groups start off until you switch them on.

## How do I change the church's name, country or colour?
For: Administrators

**Settings → Church**: church name and **Country** (sets provinces/states, postcodes, phone numbers and the privacy law).
The church's colour and page pictures are under **Settings → Appearance** (**Save the church's colour**) – everyone sees them until they choose their own under **Me → Appearance**.

## Can I change or turn off the pictures at the top of each page?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Plain pages. Page pictures. Remove the header pictures. Line art. My own colour. Themes. Appearance.

Every page has a picture at the top, in the colours of that part of the app. To choose your own, open **Me → Appearance**
and pick **Soft watercolour**, **Warm line art** or **Plain (original)**. Only you see your choice, on the device you chose it on.
To go back to your church's choice, tap **Use the church's theme**. Administrators and the Rector choose what everyone sees
until they pick their own, under **Settings → Appearance → Page pictures**.

## How do I choose my own colour for Church & Trellis?
For: Everyone
Also asked: Change the colour. My colour. Buttons colour. Themes. Appearance.

Open **Me → Appearance**, pick a colour (or any colour by its code) and tap **Save my colour**. Buttons, links and the menu
change to it, for you only, on this device. Tap **Use the church's theme** to go back to your church's colour.
Administrators and the Rector set the church's colour under **Settings → Appearance**.

## How do I use our own church words (e.g. Rector, Parishioner)?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Change "Church Partner" to "Member". Rename Pastor. Terminology.

**Settings → Terminology (Church Words)**. Choose your **Kind of church** (General, Anglican, Catholic, Methodist,
Presbyterian/Reformed, Baptist, Pentecostal, Lutheran) to fill in its words, then change any word. **Save wording**. The
screens use your words (in English); stored records don't change.

## How do I choose what each role can see?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Can members see phone numbers? Who can see what. Attendees. Access and roles.

Everything about access is on one page: **Settings → Access and roles**. From the top:
1. **Who can do what** – a card for each kind of person (Administrator, Senior Pastor, Pastors, Roster coordinators,
   Children's ministry, Church Partners, attendees): what they **see** and what they **can** do, in plain words, how many
   people have it (tap the number to see who), **View as** to see Church & Trellis as they do, and **Change**.
2. **Jobs in one part of the app** – carers, Midweek Group leaders, keepers, librarians, the church secretary for roster
   replies and the Council: who they are, and where each is chosen.
3. **Other people's contact details** – tick which details (mobile, email, home address, spouse) each kind of access sees of
   people in the church directory. Everyone sees their name and photo.
4. **Care Groups** – all groups and who is in each, which group someone is in on their page, and everyone in their own group.
5. **What Pastors can do** and 6. **Attendees** (below).
Ticks save by themselves. Point at or tap an **i** for an explanation. Administrators and Pastors always see everything.
Change one person's access on their page in People (Edit details).

## What can Pastors do? Who is the Senior Pastor (Rector)?
For: Administrators

**Settings → Access and roles → What Pastors can do**. Pastors always see and edit everyone's details, households, children and pastoral notes;
tick what else they may do (settings, giving access, ID numbers, leave, rosters, privacy, import, translations). You can choose
one **Senior Pastor**, who gets their own ticks; the others show as Assistant Pastors. Pastors get the change the next time
they open Church & Trellis. Medical notes always stay with Children's ministry.

## How do I see Church & Trellis as another role sees it?
For: Administrators, the Senior Pastor
Also asked: View as a member. Test what a roster coordinator sees.

Tap the eye next to your name (on a phone, **More**) → **View as…** and choose a role, or **View as a carer** to see a care
group the way its carer does. You stay signed in as yourself, but see only what that role may see, and nothing can be saved.
Tap **Back to Administrator** to stop.

## How do I set up services and venues?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Add a service time. Second campus. Change the 5pm service.

**Settings → Services and venues**: each regular service's name, day, time and campus or venue (leave venue empty for the
church itself). **Services that need a roster** lists upcoming services – remove one with the bin, or use **Stop holding a
regular service…**. Special services: see [rosters.md](rosters.md).

## How do emails get sent (birthdays, anniversaries, welcome)?
For: Administrators

Church & Trellis suggests emails (birthdays, anniversaries, late-spouse remembrance, newcomer welcome) only to people who agreed to
email (a married couple gets one anniversary email together). Under **Send emails using** choose your email program or the
church's Gmail (opens in the browser – be signed in there with the church's Gmail account), set the **Signed by** name, and
edit the email templates. Church & Trellis opens each email ready to send – you press send.

## How do I bring people across from my old church management system?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Import from another system. Migrate our data. Switch systems.

**Import**: export People from your old system as a CSV (include a member ID if it has one – it stops people being doubled), choose
the file, match the columns and categories, check the preview, and import. Importing again updates people rather than
doubling them. If it went wrong, remove everyone brought in and import again. Nothing is changed in your old system. We suggest running both systems side by side for a while, and we can help you transfer your data (there may be a cost, depending on the size and means of your church).

## How do I bring staff leave across from Leave Manager?
For: Administrators

**Import → From Leave Manager**: in the Google Sheet use **File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**, then choose that file.
Match staff to people, check the balances in the preview, and import. Running it again updates rather than doubles, so you
can trial it and import again at switch-over.

## How do I make "Ask in your own words" understand questions better?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Use AI for search. Gemini. The search doesn't understand my question.

Switch on AI in **Settings → Apps → AI for “Ask in your own words”**. The same switch lets **Help** write answers to people's questions
and lets Church & Trellis read photos of names (visitor cards and sign-up sheets). It needs a one-time setup of about 15 minutes in the
church's Firebase project first (the setup guide is linked there: docs/AI-SEARCH-SETUP.md), and the Information Officer
should agree, because questions go to Google's Gemini. No church records are sent – only the question (or the photo, for names, which isn't kept).

## Who is told when someone can't make a roster turn?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Church secretary roster messages. Who hears about roster replies. Add someone to roster messages.

When someone is put on the roster they get a message in **Messages**; when they say they can't make it, the roster team gets one.
Administrators and Roster coordinators always do. To add others, such as the church secretary, open **Settings → Church Services** and under
**Who hears about roster replies** type part of their name under **Add someone**.

## Church & Trellis feels slow. What can I do?
For: Administrators
Also asked: It takes ages to load. Pages are slow.

Straight after it was slow, open **About** and look at **Speed check**: how long opening took, how long each recent page took
to draw, how long the browser was too busy to respond, and how many records were loaded. Send a screenshot of it to whoever
looks after Church & Trellis. Closing other Church & Trellis tabs and using **Update now** under About can also help.

## How do I change the brochures?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Edit the brochure. Change the brochure colour. Put our phone number on the brochure. Print the brochure.

Open **About → Brochures** and choose **For our members**, **For other churches** or **For administrators** (how Church & Trellis is built and what it takes to run it).
- **Edit the wording**: click any heading or text on the brochure and type – for example your office's phone and email in
  **Need a hand?**. **Hide** takes away a block you don't want; **Show** brings it back. Changes save by themselves; tap
  **Done editing** when you've finished.
- **Language**: choose a language in the brochure editor. Wording you change is saved for that language only; the original
  brochure wording remains the fallback when you have not changed a line in that language.
- **Colour** and **Font**: Church & Trellis orange, your church's own colour (Settings → Appearance) or any colour, and a choice of fonts.
- **Print or save as PDF**: A4 landscape, both sides, flipping on the short edge, then fold in three.
If a red warning says a panel doesn't fit, shorten its text or hide a block. **Back to the original wording** clears wording
changes for the language you selected; **Back to Church & Trellis's colour and font** resets the design. When Church & Trellis updates
a brochure's wording, any change you made to that line is listed so you can redo it. Only Administrators can open or change
the editor; its saved wording is kept separately from general church settings.

## How do I set up Church & Trellis at another church?
For: Administrators
Also asked: Install Church & Trellis for a new church. Start using Church & Trellis. A friend's church wants it. Setup guide.

Open the setup tool: **https://app.churchandtrellis.com/site/setup.html** (also linked from the website as **Set up Church
Hub for your church**). Use a computer. It goes one step at a time – the church's own Google account, its copy of the website,
its database, the first sign-in, then privacy, wording, services and staff – filling in each instruction with that church's
own names, with copy buttons and links to the right pages. It remembers where you stopped. Never type passwords into it.

## What else can our church switch on?
For: Administrators
Also asked: What haven't we set up yet? What's new for Administrators? Setup checklist.

The setup tool keeps a list for each church it set up: **Everything else Church & Trellis can do** – Care Groups, Midweek Groups,
rosters, message groups, leave, the library, AI search and more – with **How**, **Done** and **Not for us**, and **New** beside
anything added since you last looked. It also reminds you about once a month to bring the latest Church & Trellis into your
church's copy. The list is kept on the computer you used; **More → Save progress to a file** carries it to another.

## How do I check which version we're on?
For: Everyone

**About** shows the version and date, **What's new**, and **Check for updates**. Administrators also find the printable
**Brochures** there (for members and for other churches).
